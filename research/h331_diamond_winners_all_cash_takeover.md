# H331 — Diamond Winners all-cash `Every Ticket Wins` full-takeover bound

Checked: 2026-08-28
State: **CLOSED / ALL-CASH FLOOR BUT ECONOMICS BELOW BREAK-EVEN**

## Why this packet is new

H329 and H330 closed `Every Ticket Wins` constructions whose guaranteed outcomes were site credit or downstream entries rather than withdrawable cash. H331 tests the stronger case the previous gate asked for: **every identifier itself pays cash**, and the published per-person cap equals the entire finite pool.

The operator page for the finished Diamond Winners competition states:
- 19,999 tickets total;
- £0.59 per entry;
- every ticket wins a cash prize from 5p to £500;
- max entries per person = 19,999;
- separate £150 end prize.

## Exact takeover arithmetic

At the published online price, complete ownership would cost:

`19,999 × £0.59 = £11,799.41`.

Because every identifier has a deterministic cash instant prize, complete ownership would collect every instant liability: **£6,503.30**. Grant the takeover the entire separate £150 end prize as well. The strongest possible deterministic gross is therefore:

`£6,503.30 + £150 = £6,653.30`.

Return on acquisition cost:

`£6,653.30 / £11,799.41 = 56.386718%`.

Deficit:

`£11,799.41 - £6,653.30 = £5,146.11`.

The exact break-even effective ticket price for a full takeover would be only **£0.332681634**. Relative to the listed £0.59 price, an additional deterministic discount of more than **43.613282%** would be required before this particular all-cash pool could cross break-even.

## Postal route

The page also offers free postal entry, but each free entry must be sent separately and acceptance before the cap/closing time is not guaranteed. H331 does not need postal-cost assumptions to close the published online construction: the all-cash finite-pool economics already fail decisively at the listed price. Any future reopening must use a separately proved deterministic effective acquisition cost below £0.332681634 per accepted ID.

## Conclusion

This is a useful structural checkpoint: **positive cash on every terminal identifier plus a full-pool person cap is still insufficient**. Once the entire finite payout vector is known, the correct gate is simply total deterministic liabilities versus exact total acquisition cost.

H331 is closed under the checked terms. It is not a profitable strategy.

## Reopen condition

Reopen only if the same or analogous all-cash finite pool has a documented deterministic discount/subsidy that lowers effective accepted-entry cost below the exact break-even price, while preserving the ability of one player to acquire every relevant identifier before outsiders can block the takeover.
