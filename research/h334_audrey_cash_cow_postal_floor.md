## Objective
Follow H333's corrected target directly: find a live/current competition where every accepted free/postal identifier pays withdrawable cash strictly above compliant marginal acquisition cost.

## Live product
Audrey Competitions currently lists `Cash Cow - Every Ticket Wins!` with 15,125 total identifiers. Snapshot checked 2026-08-28: 2,840 sold.

- instant cash = `6*300+6*200+6*100+10*50+12*25+15*15+20*10+50*5+15000*1 = £20,075`;
- minimum cash on every identifier = **£1**;
- arithmetic missing/inconclusive = **0**.

## Free postal route
The competition page states that a free entry can be sent on a separate unenclosed A5 postcard. Required identifying details must match a registered account; each free entry must be posted separately and received before the closing date/cap. Audrey's current Terms state that qualifying free-route instant-win entrants are randomly allocated an entry number.

Because all 15,125 IDs carry at least £1 cash, random allocation does not reduce the cash floor. External entrants and full-pool takeover are irrelevant.

## Stamp-only economics
Current Royal Mail Second Class postage benchmark used by the project: **£0.91**.

Conditional on a compliant entry being accepted into Cash Cow:
- minimum cash = £1.00;
- stamp cost = £0.91;
- stamp-only net = **+£0.09**;
- gross = **109.8901098901%**;
- therefore every additional required real cost must total **< £0.09** for strict positive net profit.

This is a live/current version of the H333 arithmetic mechanism.

## Execution blockers
The candidate is not certified as SUCCESS yet.

1. **All-in physical compliance cost:** the route requires an unenclosed A5 postcard, so card stock plus writing/printing/fulfilment must fit inside the remaining 9p margin. Retail A5 card examples often exceed that margin. Bulk print/mail offers can fall below £1 all-in, but that alone is not enough because Audrey Terms reject automatically computer-generated entries.
2. **Acceptance / conduct discretion:** Audrey reserves rights to disqualify conduct contrary to the spirit/intention of the prize competition. A scaled machine-generated/postal-house implementation therefore cannot be treated as unconditionally valid without stronger evidence.
3. **Eligibility:** entry is limited to UK residents aged 18+. Any eventual executable certificate must state that condition explicitly.

## Result
H334 is materially stronger than H333 because the qualifying all-cash competition is live and its free route is published contemporaneously. The arithmetic layer is exact and zero-inconclusive. The only remaining uncertainty is execution/compliance: can an eligible entrant produce and send each qualifying A5 postcard for total marginal cost under £1 using a method clearly allowed by the Terms?
