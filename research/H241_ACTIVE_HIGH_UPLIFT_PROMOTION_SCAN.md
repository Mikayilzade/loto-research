# H241 — Active high-uplift Keno promotion scan

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: NOT SUCCESS

## Target
Search for an active or already-announced Keno promotion whose deterministic, pre-purchase-known uplift is large enough to clear the H240 full-coverage break-even threshold (~+53.34% on the verified Ohio 3-spot reference economics), ideally a free universal 2X layer.

## Current official-source scan

### Ohio
The current Ohio Lottery KENO page still describes the normal paid BOOSTER feature and lists 2026 promotions, but the currently surfaced 2026 promotion is the Bracket Challenge rather than a KENO Double BOOSTER event. The official September 2025 KENO Double BOOSTER rules remain useful historical proof that all qualifying BOOSTERS can be doubled in a fixed window, but that event is expired.

No active or announced August/September 2026 all-ticket KENO doubler was found in this run.

### Georgia
Georgia's 2026 KENO Bonus Hours gave +30% payouts during specified June/July windows. The final listed window was July 15, 2026, so it is expired and below the H240 +53.34% free-uplift threshold anyway.

### North Carolina
North Carolina's 2026 Keno Bonus Hours gave +50% on winning tickets with Multiplier during Fridays in June and July. It is expired. It also required adding Multiplier, which doubles the base ticket cost, so the nominal +50% bonus is not equivalent to a free +50% overlay on base wagers.

### Rhode Island
Searches for a 2026 recurrence of `Lucky 3 Spot Keno` found the 2024 and 2025 official rules but no active or announced 2026 recurrence. The historical mechanism is exactly the desired class: qualifying 3-spot tickets bought at the specified venue/time carried a printed message that any prize would be doubled, with no separate Keno Plus purchase stated in the rules.

The currently active 2026 RI `Kick Back with Keno` promo remains only a $1 bonus per $5 qualifying purchase (nominal 20%), as analyzed in H240.

## Result
No active or already-announced promotion found in the checked official sources as of 2026-08-24 supplies a deterministic free uplift >=53.34% to every required 3-spot wager.

This is not evidence that none exists anywhere; it is a negative result for the jurisdictions/mechanisms checked in this run. The most promising recurring pattern remains RI's historical Lucky 3 Spot 2X promotion because it targeted exactly 3-spot Keno and did not state an extra multiplier purchase requirement.
