# H021 — forced-distribution / external-subsidy bound

Updated: 2026-08-15
Status: **general necessary-condition screen implemented; UK Lotto Must-Be-Won and Austrian LottoPlus promotion fail as standalone guaranteed full-space constructions under current verified parameters**

## Question
Can a lottery become a guaranteed-profit buy-the-pot opportunity merely because a jackpot or fixed promotional pool is forced to be distributed?

Answer: **not by itself**. Forced distribution removes one failure mode (money rolling away), but a full-space portfolio still pays the operator takeout on every line. A terminal guarantee requires enough *guaranteed external subsidy* to overcome that takeout, and it must remain capturable after sharing, caps, taxes, execution limits and external entries.

## Necessary-condition bound
Let:
- `S` = our total acquisition spend;
- `r` = fraction of our spend that can return to the prize pool under the game's payout allocation, `0 <= r <= 1`;
- `B` = guaranteed external money already committed to the relevant draw(s), excluding money generated by our own purchases;
- `E` = guaranteed prize-pool contribution from external players that our portfolio is guaranteed to capture net of sharing (normally zero unless rules provide such a floor);
- `C` = taxes, fees, execution and other unavoidable costs.

Even under the deliberately favorable assumption that every dollar/euro/pound of the prize pool attributable to our own play returns to us, terminal guaranteed profit requires

`r*S + B + E - C > S`

or equivalently

`B + E > (1-r)*S + C`.

Thus `(1-r)*S` is the **minimum guaranteed external subsidy requirement** before sharing/friction.

For a strict all-outcome guarantee, ordinary external sales cannot be counted as `E` unless the rules guarantee both their minimum amount and our minimum capture share. If there is a legal state with no external sales, or external winners can dilute the pool without a hard bound, `E=0` is the correct guarantee-screen value.

This is a necessary condition, not sufficient. Passing it would still require exact prize-allocation, sharing, tax and execution proofs.

## 1. UK Lotto Must-Be-Won
Current official parameters verified:
- choose 6 of 59;
- £2 per play;
- full combination space `C(59,6)=45,057,474`;
- full-space spend `S = £90,114,948`;
- official procedures state that **on average 50% of Lotto sales** is available for Lotto prizes, with part of that entering a reserve fund;
- jackpot rolls five times; if the jackpot is not won in the fifth rollover draw, it is redistributed to lower cash tiers under the published Must-Be-Won percentages.

Sources:
- National Lottery Lotto game procedures: https://www.national-lottery.co.uk/games/lotto/game-procedures
- National Lottery Lotto play/about pages: https://www.national-lottery.co.uk/games/lotto/about-lotto

### Screening threshold
Using the maximally favorable `r=0.50` as though the entire average prize allocation generated by our lines could be guaranteed back to us:

`required guaranteed external subsidy > (1-0.50) * £90,114,948`

`> £45,057,474` before execution/tax/sharing.

This is already much larger than the ordinary Must-Be-Won jackpot magnitudes visible in official draw history (e.g. £15m Must-Be-Won-scale draws appear in the history). More importantly, the 50% figure is an average allocation, not a per-draw contractual floor to our portfolio, so this screen is intentionally favorable and **cannot itself certify profit even if crossed**.

### Guarantee conclusion
Must-Be-Won mechanics ensure jackpot money is distributed, but they do not guarantee a subsidy remotely large enough to cover the full-space takeout, nor a guaranteed capture of external-player contributions. Full-space also introduces many winning entries while outside players may create additional winners.

**UK Lotto Must-Be-Won is rejected as a standalone full-space guaranteed-profit construction under the verified current structure.** It remains an EV/state overlay only.

## 2. Austrian Lotto + LottoPlus forced-distribution promotion
Current official parameters verified on 2026-08-15:
- Lotto 6/45 base tip = €1.50;
- from 6 July 2026, LottoPlus costs €0.80 per Lotto tip and enters the same tip into **two** LottoPlus draws;
- announced payout shares changed to **48% for Lotto** and **45% for LottoPlus**;
- LottoPlus has no jackpot carryover: if no six-number winner exists, the top-tier money is distributed to five-number winners;
- the 2 August 2026 promotion fixed **€500,000 in the six-number tier in each of the two LottoPlus draws**, €1,000,000 total, and stated that the million would be paid out even if there were no six-number winners.

Sources:
- Austrian Lotteries current Lotto page: https://www.lotterien.at/spiele/lotteriespiele/lotto-6-aus-45
- 28 May 2026 LottoPlus rules/pricing update: https://www.lotterien.at/pressemitteilung/wer-ab-juli-bei-lottoplus-mitspielt-nimmt-automatisch-an-zwei-ziehungen-pro-runde-teil
- 23 July 2026 fixed-pool promotion: https://www.lotterien.at/pressemitteilung/bei-lottoplus-jetzt-insgesamt-1-mio-euro-zu-gewinnen

### Full-space acquisition
`C(45,6)=8,145,060` lines.

Because LottoPlus is an add-on to the Lotto base play, complete coverage with LottoPlus requires at least

`S = 8,145,060 * (€1.50 + €0.80) = €18,733,638`.

The player-funded payout allocation per covered line, using the published aggregate payout ratios, is at most

`€1.50*0.48 + €0.80*0.45 = €1.08`.

Across the space this equals

`€8,796,664.80`.

Takeout gap:

`€18,733,638 - €8,796,664.80 = €9,936,973.20`.

Even granting the entire advertised **€1,000,000** fixed LottoPlus promotion as genuinely external subsidy and assuming our portfolio captures all of it:

remaining gap `= €8,936,973.20` before any sharing, tax or execution cost.

The promotion therefore misses the minimum subsidy threshold by almost €8.94m in the zero-external-sales guarantee state.

### Guarantee conclusion
The forced distribution is real and economically useful, but the fixed subsidy is far too small relative to complete acquisition cost and takeout.

**This current/recent LottoPlus fixed-pool structure is rejected as a guaranteed-profit full-space strategy.**

## 3. Strategic implication
A large family of tempting 'final draw / must be won / guaranteed pool' ideas can now be screened before expensive combinatorial work.

For any candidate full-space strategy:
1. compute full acquisition `S`;
2. obtain the strongest defensible player-funded payout fraction `r`;
3. compute the takeout hurdle `(1-r)S`;
4. count only legally guaranteed external subsidy/capture as `B+E`;
5. reject immediately if `B+E <= (1-r)S + costs`;
6. if it passes, only then solve sharing, prize-tier allocation, ticket limits, taxes and execution.

This does **not** prove that all future forced-distribution lotteries are impossible. It sharply narrows the search to rare states where an external carryover, promotion, overlay or stale fixed pool is enormous relative to the combination-space acquisition cost.
