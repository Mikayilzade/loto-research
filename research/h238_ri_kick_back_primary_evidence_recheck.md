# H238 — Rhode Island Kick Back with Keno primary-evidence recheck

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: NOT SUCCESS / DATA-BLOCKED
Scope: lottery-only promotion/execution check.

## Question
Does the currently advertised Rhode Island `Kick Back with Keno Promotion` expose a deterministic pre-purchase rebate/multiplier or other entitlement strong enough to reopen the H236 full-space guarantee path?

## Fresh primary evidence
1. The official Rhode Island Lottery homepage currently advertises `Kick Back with Keno Promotion` as an active promotion (fresh crawl on 2026-08-24).
2. The current official 2026 Rhode Island Lottery rules still define ordinary Keno Plus as a separate paid feature whose multiplier (`No Plus`, `2X`, `3X`, `4X`, `5X`, `10X`) is determined by a drawing immediately before the Keno draw. Therefore ordinary Keno Plus is not a player-selectable deterministic 2X entitlement.
3. The current 2026 rules also define Keno Overtime as another separately purchased random follow-on feature; Keno Plus does not apply to Overtime wins.
4. Fresh searches of the official RILOT domain did not recover a current `Kick Back with Keno` rules PDF/page containing the promotion's exact award schedule, transaction threshold, time window, cancellation treatment, issuance control, cap, or whether the reward is cash / free play / ticket credit.

## Consequence
The active-promotion fact is now primary-source verified, but the mechanics required by PROJECT_RULES for a strict guarantee are still absent. The historical Lucky 3 Spot 2X mechanics from H236 cannot be imported into the current promotion. No deterministic cash-equivalent floor can be assigned to `Kick Back with Keno` from the recovered current primary evidence.

Thus the current promotion does **not** presently supply an executable all-outcome positive-profit strategy, and it must remain data-blocked rather than guessed.

## Reopen gate
Reopen only if a materially new current primary source establishes at least:
- deterministic entitlement formula known before purchase;
- exact qualifying spend / ticket constraints;
- cash or cash-equivalent redemption value;
- per-player / per-transaction / per-day limits;
- cancellation/refund interaction;
- promotion dates/hours and channel restrictions;
- no random issuance branch that can leave the entitlement at zero.

**NOT SUCCESS.** Current `Kick Back with Keno` existence is verified, but current primary rules sufficient to prove a deterministic promotion floor were not recovered. H236's historical 2X entitlement cannot be assumed for this 2026 promotion.
