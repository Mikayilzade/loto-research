# CHECKED PROJECTS / TESTS — H302 APPEND

## H302 — SC Competitions free-postal finite-pool takeover

**Status:** CLOSED / EXECUTION-BLOCKED.

Tested a new mechanism: replace paid finite-pool acquisition with the operator's free postal entry route, pricing the unavoidable entry cost at current Royal Mail 2nd Class postage.

Observed economics for the 100-entry / £100-cash format:
- paid full pool: £199;
- 100 compliant 2nd Class postcards at £0.91 each: £91;
- conditional arithmetic surplus if all 100 are accepted and no external entry exists: £9;
- mixed full acquisition first becomes conditionally positive at 92 postal + 8 paid entries = £99.64.

Rejected as a rigorous guarantee because postal dispatch does not reserve an Entry Number. SC terms expressly deny proof-of-posting as proof of receipt, allow per-raffle entrant caps, and leave a legal path for delayed/rejected postal entries or external paid entries to consume finite capacity first. Under-allocation can also change the advertised-prize treatment.

**Do not reopen** ordinary unreserved postal-entry takeover merely because face-value arithmetic exceeds postage. Reopen only with deterministic reservation/acknowledgement of the full relevant finite pool or another mechanism that keeps the worst-case floor positive despite external entries.
