# H279 EXECUTION CHECK — Kentucky Pick 3 online acquisition

Follow-up after the exact +20% matched-deposit theorem.

## What improved

The Kentucky Lottery's live online Pick 3 page exposes **Add to cart** and **Buy Now** and states that the player is not charged until purchase is confirmed in the next step. Current Pick 3 rules also confirm that iLottery purchase is supported and that the $0.50 minimum wager applies online, although online wager options/features may differ from retail.

This makes a multi-selection checkout path plausible rather than requiring 100 unrelated payment operations.

## Why execution is still not certified

The public material found does **not** establish that a cart containing the entire 00-99 Front Pair cover is validated and committed atomically on an all-or-none basis. The official Pick 3 rules additionally retain a prize-liability cutoff that may stop sales when a wager would push liability for a possible result above the internal limit, and that limit need not be publicly announced.

The general iLottery terms also reserve the right to refuse an attempted purchase for any reason.

Therefore an online cart is useful evidence but not enough to prove that every one of the 100 Pair plays will necessarily be accepted for one drawing. A split/partial acceptance state cannot be excluded from the public documentation reviewed so far.

## Current execution verdict

**STILL DATA-BLOCKED, BUT NARROWED:** arithmetic and wallet conversion are rigorous; remaining unknown is all-lines acceptance/atomicity under Pick 3 liability controls.

The smallest high-value construction remains 100 Front Pair wagers at $0.50. It adds only $30 of liability to each possible three-digit result, materially smaller than the 1,000-Straight construction's $300 uniform liability increment, so it should be prioritized for any future operational/terms verification.
