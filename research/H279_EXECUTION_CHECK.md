# H279 EXECUTION CHECK — Kentucky Pick 3 online acquisition

Follow-up after the exact +20% matched-deposit theorem.

## What improved

The Kentucky Lottery's live online Pick 3 page exposes **Add to cart** and **Buy Now** and states that the player is not charged until purchase is confirmed in the next step. Current Pick 3 rules also confirm that iLottery purchase is supported and that online wager options/features may differ from retail.

## Binding execution blocker now established

Current Kentucky iLottery Terms of Use (effective June 2026, v12.1) expressly state that KLC:
- does **not guarantee the timeliness of any purchase** and may refuse an attempted purchase for any reason;
- may **limit purchases of any game and/or wager on a particular set of numbers at any time and without notice**.

The Pick 3 rules independently allow KLC to establish a **prize-liability limit without public notice** and cut off sales for a drawing if liability would be exceeded by particular numbers.

These are not merely missing UI details. They mean that public rules do not create an enforceable pre-draw guarantee that all 100 Front Pair selections 00-99 will be accepted for one drawing, even if the UI permits a multi-selection cart. A partial accepted subset has no deterministic positive floor.

## Current execution verdict

**CLOSED / EXECUTION-BLOCKED under the project guarantee standard.**

Arithmetic remains valid conditionally:
- 100 Front Pair wagers at $0.50 consume $50 wallet;
- a $25 eligible first deposit plus $25 matched Bonus funds that wallet;
- if all 100 pair wagers are accepted for the same drawing, exactly one wins $30, for +$5 versus cash deposited.

But complete acquisition cannot be guaranteed from the governing public rules because KLC retains unilateral no-notice purchase/number restrictions and refusal rights. Reopen H279 only if binding later evidence establishes an all-or-none reservation/acceptance mechanism overriding those restrictions for the complete 00-99 cover.
