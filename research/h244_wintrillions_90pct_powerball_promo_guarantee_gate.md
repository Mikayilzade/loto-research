# H244 — WinTrillions 90% Powerball promotion guarantee gate

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: REJECTED FOR STRICT GUARANTEE
Scope: LOTTERY-ADJACENT PROMOTIONAL OVERLAY ONLY

## Question
Could a heavily discounted Powerball offer become a true guaranteed-profit full-space strategy by reducing effective ticket cost below the deterministic fixed-prize floor?

## Current evidence
Third-party pages currently indexed in August 2026 advertise a WinTrillions new-player offer described as a 90% discount on a monthly US Powerball subscription. One detailed offer description states a monthly subscription means 8 consecutive draws and quotes a promotional price of EUR 4.40 for 8 Powerball entries. This is not treated as primary proof of a universally available 2026 offer; it is only a lead.

The current WinTrillions Terms and Conditions are primary operator evidence. They define subscriptions and a ticket-order / betting structure, reserve promotion eligibility discretion, prohibit commercial/professional use, and contain a decisive anti-guarantee clause: where enhanced promotional payments create guaranteed player profits irrespective of outcome, the operator reserves the right to ignore the bonus element and settle without it. The same terms also permit transaction handling fees up to 10% and contain discretionary account/promotion controls.

## Exact Powerball full-space counterfactual
Current Powerball state space:

- C(69,5) * 26 = 292,201,338 combinations
- ordinary $2 full-space spend = $584,402,676

Using the current fixed prize schedule and excluding the jackpot entirely, exact deterministic lower-tier gross over full coverage is:

- 5 white, no Powerball: 25 * $1,000,000
- 4 + Powerball: 320 * $50,000
- 4 only: 8,000 * $100
- 3 + Powerball: 20,160 * $100
- 3 only: 504,000 * $7
- 2 + Powerball: 416,640 * $7
- 1 + Powerball: 3,176,880 * $4
- 0 + Powerball: 7,624,512 * $4

Total deterministic non-jackpot gross = **$93,466,048**.

A hypothetical universal 90% discount would reduce full-space cost to **$58,440,267.60**, creating a deterministic pre-jackpot margin of **+$35,025,780.40**. Therefore this discount magnitude is mathematically strong enough in principle: if contractually irrevocable, unlimited, scalable to all combinations and attached to genuine payable Powerball-equivalent claims, it would clear the guarantee threshold without relying on jackpot sharing.

## Why it still fails terminal SUCCESS
The operator contract itself defeats the guarantee condition. Current WinTrillions Terms state that when a series of bets, because of enhanced payments through promotional offers, produces guaranteed player profits irrespective of outcome, the operator may ignore the bonus element and settle at the non-promotional terms.

That adverse branch is exactly the branch a full-space 90%-discount construction would rely on. Once the discount may be removed specifically because the strategy guarantees profit, the worst-case contractual cost reverts toward ordinary pricing and the deterministic lower-tier floor is far below spend.

Additional blockers strengthen the rejection:

1. the 90% offer is currently evidenced through third-party promotional pages rather than a recovered current operator-specific offer page with exact caps;
2. the detailed indexed offer appears to be a small monthly-subscription welcome offer (8 draws), not proof of unlimited line-level discounting;
3. WinTrillions terms reserve promotion eligibility and anti-abuse discretion;
4. commercial/professional use is prohibited;
5. transaction handling fees may apply;
6. the service can involve hedged bets / ticket-order alternatives rather than direct player ownership of a physical US ticket.

## Verdict
**REJECTED FOR STRICT GUARANTEE.** The economics are unusually strong: a genuinely irrevocable universal 90% Powerball discount would make the fixed lower-tier full-space floor profitable before jackpot. But the current operator Terms expressly preserve a contractual branch that removes promotional enhancement when it creates guaranteed profit, so no all-outcome enforceable guarantee exists.

## Reopen rule
Reopen only if a lottery operator or intermediary publishes a promotion that is simultaneously:

- deterministic and irrevocable after qualification;
- large enough to push deterministic fixed-prize full-space payout above net acquisition cost;
- scalable to the required combination set;
- not subject to anti-guaranteed-profit, anti-arbitrage, abuse, discretionary cancellation or line caps that destroy the proof;
- legally executable from the relevant jurisdiction;
- payable after all fees, taxes and settlement rules.
