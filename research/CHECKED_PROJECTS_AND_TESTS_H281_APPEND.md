## H281 — Virginia current free-game promotional floor

**Mechanism tested:** deterministic promotional entitlement of ten free Jackpot Spectacular games after first mobile ticket cashing, including whether it can act as the missing subsidy for an exact Virginia Pick 3 Pair cover.

**Evidence checked:**
- Virginia Lottery current Mobile Ticket Cashing page;
- Virginia Lottery Jackpot Spectacular game page;
- Virginia Lottery Pick 3 current prize table;
- Virginia Lottery account Terms and Conditions.

**Exact tests:**
1. Enumerated Pair outcome space size: 100 ordered two-digit values.
2. Verified one-copy full Pair cover cost/prize identity at both allowed base stakes: 50% fixed gross return.
3. Derived strict subsidy hurdles: >$25 for the $0.50 cover and >$50 for the $1 cover.
4. Tested promotional award at the worst-case rather than EV layer.
5. Published 1-in-3.99 any-prize odds establish legal non-winning Jackpot Spectacular outcomes; no minimum aggregate win is promised across ten promotional games.
6. Therefore promotional strict cash floor = $0 and cannot clear either exact-cover deficit.

**Result:** **CLOSED** for strict guaranteed-profit use of this free-game mechanism. Expected promotional value is irrelevant to the guarantee target because a legal all-losing bonus realization remains.

**Reopen condition:** binding positive minimum aggregate prize or deterministic withdrawable cash/credit exceeding the exact cover deficit.
