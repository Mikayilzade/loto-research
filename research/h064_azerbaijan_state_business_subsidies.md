# H064 — Azerbaijan statutory/state-backed business subsidies

Updated: 2026-08-17
Status: **multiple deterministic incremental subsidies validated; standalone guaranteed-profit path rejected for screened mechanisms**

## Goal
Search current Azerbaijan state-backed programs for a subsidy that creates a strictly positive cash floor under objective/formulaic eligibility, without relying on lottery/random selection, uncontrolled customer demand, or a competitive grant decision.

The key distinction is between:
- **incremental subsidy on an independently economic activity**; and
- **standalone arbitrage**, where subsidy cash itself exceeds every required cost on every allowed branch.

## General reimbursement theorem
If a program reimburses a fraction `r` of a qualifying cost `C`, with `0 <= r <= 1`, and provides no separate unconditional cash payment, then isolated cash result is:

`net = r*C - C = -(1-r)C <= 0`.

Therefore percentage reimbursement alone cannot create strictly positive standalone profit. At `r=100%` the best isolated result is zero before overhead/time; at `r<100%` it is a deterministic loss unless the funded activity has independent economic value.

This theorem is used below to close several program classes without confusing cost reduction with cash arbitrage.

## 1. Wage co-financing / social workplaces — strong deterministic incremental subsidy
Current DOST guidance states that eligible private employers may hire persons referred by the State Employment Agency/DOST into social jobs. Employer eligibility includes being non-state/non-municipal, having operated during the last year, and having a vacancy/new vacancy in priority economic fields/professions.

The unemployment-insurance fund finances **50%** of:
- the position salary; and
- specified payroll deductions/charges calculated from the payroll fund.

The funded portion is capped by the prior-year average monthly nominal salary. The program is contractual and time-limited; current 2026 Ministry reporting says the salary support runs for **1 year**. Each employer is also subject to employee-count limits.

Primary sources:
- DOST, `İşçilərin əməkhaqlarının işəgötürənlə birgə maliyyələşdirilməsi`;
- Ministry of Labour, 6 April 2026 programme update.

### Guarantee test
Let covered salary + covered employer charges be `C`.

State funding = `0.5*C`.
Employer still bears at least `0.5*C`, plus any non-covered bonuses/overtime/other costs.

Thus the subsidy is **deterministic incremental employer savings after eligibility/contract**, but it is not standalone profit. Profit depends on the independent economic value of the employee's work.

Status: **VALIDATED incremental subsidy; REJECTED standalone guarantee**.

## 2. 2026 vulnerable-group job-creation pilot — up to 100% payroll for first 3 months
A January 28, 2026 Ministry of Labour / World Bank pilot for selected Employment Support Project beneficiaries covers:
- **100% of wages plus employer/employee taxes and social contributions for the first 3 months**;
- **50% for the following 3 months**.

The target population is beneficiaries who created small businesses under the Employment Support Project and then hire employees; the pilot is not a generally open automatic employer entitlement.

Primary source:
- Ministry of Labour, `Additional support for participants of the Employment Support Project and for the creation of jobs for vulnerable groups`, 28 January 2026.

### Guarantee test
For the first 3 months the direct covered payroll cash burden can fall to zero, which is stronger than ordinary 50% co-financing. But no separate excess cash bounty is stated: support equals covered payroll/taxes. Any positive profit still depends on value produced by the employee/business, while admission itself is pilot/program-specific.

Status: **VALIDATED near-zero direct payroll-cost mechanism; NOT standalone cash-profit guarantee**.

## 3. Liberated-territories social-insurance subsidy
The State Tax Service describes a statutory social-insurance subsidy for qualifying non-state/non-oil-gas activity in liberated territories.

For employers with employees:
- 2023–2025: 100% of paid mandatory state social insurance;
- **2026–2028: 80%**;
- 2029–2030: 60%;
- 2031–2032: 40%.

For qualifying self-employed individuals without hired employees, including specified fixed-receipt/distinguishing-sign activities, the published mechanism provides **100% subsidy of their own mandatory state social-insurance contributions for 10 years**, subject to statutory exclusions.

Primary sources:
- State Tax Service: `İşğaldan azad edilmiş ərazilərdə fəaliyyət göstərən sahibkarlara daha bir güzəşt`;
- State Tax Service micro-entrepreneur guidance.

### Guarantee test
Employer route in 2026: `r=0.8`, so isolated cash result on the contribution is `-0.2*C` before all other business costs.

Self-employed 100% route: `r=1.0`, so isolated contribution cycle is at best `0` net before time/compliance/other costs.

Status: **VALIDATED deterministic cost subsidy; REJECTED standalone positive-cash guarantee**.

## 4. Fixed 600-AZN relocation/employment allowance in liberated territories
DOST currently states that a specialist meeting the statutory liberated-territory employment conditions receives a **600 AZN one-time allowance** to support initial material/domestic conditions. The service passport requires, among other things:
- an employment contract for work in the liberated territories for at least one year;
- at least 24 months of work experience during the previous 60 months;
- the employer/workplace/settlement conditions specified by Labour Code Article 21-1.

The benefit is assigned proactively once conditions are met.

Primary sources:
- DOST `Birdəfəlik müavinətlər`;
- DOST service passport for the liberated-territory specialist allowance.

### Guarantee test
This is a genuine **fixed cash transfer**, unlike percentage reimbursement. However it is not a zero-state arbitrage: obtaining and performing a qualifying one-year job, relocating/settling, and preserving eligibility are substantive uncontrolled obligations. The 600 AZN does not hard-bound those costs and cannot establish positive net profit from entering solely to capture the allowance.

Status: **FIXED CASH MECHANISM VALIDATED; terminal guarantee rejected due job/relocation obligation and uncontrolled cost floor**.

## 5. KOBIA internal market research support
KOBIA states:
- micro enterprises: **80%** of each eligible internal market-research cost funded;
- small enterprises: **50%** funded;
- support amount up to **20,000 AZN** per research;
- medium enterprises receive non-cash training/information support under this mechanism.

Primary source:
- KOBIA `Daxili bazar araşdırması`.

### Guarantee test
Micro route leaves at least 20% of research cost with the entrepreneur; small route leaves 50%. The support is tied to the research expenditure, not an unconditional excess cash award.

Status: **REJECTED standalone guarantee by reimbursement theorem; useful business subsidy**.

## 6. Export-support reimbursements
The Ministry of Economy states that registered exporters of qualifying non-oil Azerbaijani-origin products may use export-support measures financed from the state budget. For at least one documented support route, support is **50% of incurred expenses, capped at 10,000 AZN**.

Primary source:
- Ministry of Economy `Promotion of exports and investments` FAQ.

### Guarantee test
A 50% expense reimbursement cannot by itself create positive standalone cash profit; it reduces an independently incurred export/certification/promotion cost.

Status: **REJECTED standalone guarantee; incremental export-cost reduction only**.

## 7. Startup Certificate tax exemption
KOBIA confirms that micro/small businesses receiving a Startup Certificate are exempt from profit/income tax on qualifying innovation income for **3 years**.

However issuance is based on Expert Council assessment of innovation/competitiveness and is not automatic. More importantly, the benefit applies only after qualifying income/profit exists.

Status: **REJECTED standalone guarantee; potentially strong tax optimization on already profitable innovation activity**.

## 8. Investment incentive certificate
The Ministry of Economy / KOBIA describe a 7-year package for qualifying investment projects including:
- land/property tax exemptions;
- customs-duty and VAT exemptions on qualifying machinery/equipment imports;
- 50% discount on profit/income tax for covered projects.

The mechanism requires a qualifying project and documented investment (including evidence that 10% of the prescribed project amount has been invested for the application route).

Status: **REJECTED standalone guarantee; cost/tax reduction conditional on real investment and taxable activity**.

## 9. KOBIA grants up to 20,000 AZN
KOBIA grant competitions can finance eligible education/science/research/support projects up to **20,000 AZN**.

This is not a strict pre-commitment guarantee because:
- projects are competitively evaluated by an Expert Commission;
- funding is project-specific rather than an unconditional withdrawable reward;
- historical competitions have many more applicants than winners.

Status: **REJECTED guarantee due competitive-selection gate**.

## Strategic conclusion
H064 found several of the strongest deterministic Azerbaijan state/business subsidies in the project so far, but none is a standalone guaranteed-profit arbitrage.

The strongest mechanisms screened are:
1. 50% wage/payroll co-financing for contracted social jobs;
2. 2026 pilot covering 100% of payroll+taxes for 3 months, then 50% for 3 months;
3. 80% employer social-insurance subsidy in liberated territories during 2026–2028;
4. 100% self-employed social-insurance subsidy in covered liberated-territory cases;
5. fixed **600 AZN** liberated-territory specialist allowance;
6. 80% micro-enterprise internal-market-research reimbursement;
7. 50% export-support reimbursement;
8. tax/customs exemptions for certified startup/investment activity.

All percentage reimbursements either equal or underpay the underlying cost. The only separately fixed cash item found in this packet (600 AZN) requires a qualifying one-year employment/relocation state whose costs are not bounded below the payment. Competitive grants fail the pre-commitment entitlement gate.

Therefore **no screened H064 mechanism proves a strictly positive standalone cash floor from zero-state entry**.

## Next research implication
The remaining higher-value state/business classes should focus only on structures with a **separate fixed cash payment in excess of required incremental cost**, such as:
- per-hire cash bounties paid in addition to wage reimbursement;
- fixed relocation/creation grants with objectively automatic eligibility and a hard-bounded obligation cost;
- refundable tax credits exceeding tax liability;
- scrappage/energy/digitalization rebates where payout can exceed a hard-bounded acquisition/resale loss;
- producer/export bonuses calculated per independently sold unit where the underlying sale is already contractually owed.

Percentage cost reimbursements `r<=100%` can now be deprioritized as standalone terminal candidates unless paired with a separate deterministic cash component.
