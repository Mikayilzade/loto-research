## Exact full-space acquisition cost
Because A and B are independent 4-of-20 boards:

`M = C(20,4)^2 = 4,845^2 = 23,474,025` base variants.

At 2 AZN each:

`S = 23,474,025 × 2 = 46,948,050 AZN`.

This portfolio necessarily contains the realized 4+4 winning line exactly once, so outcome escape is eliminated. The remaining issue is economics and sharing.

## Sales-linked statutory subsidy theorem
Let:
- `S` = our incremental full-space spend;
- `r` = fraction of current sales entering the current draw's ordinary prize fund;
- `B` = all **pre-existing** value carried into this draw from prior draws/external transfers and genuinely available to our winning portfolio;
- `C` = taxes, execution, retailer/system overhead and other costs.

If current prize funding is sales-linked, the law caps the relevant sales-linked prize-fund parameter at `r <= 0.80`.

Even under the deliberately strongest player-favorable assumptions:
1. every AZN of the current prize fund generated by our own purchase eventually returns to our portfolio;
2. no outside player takes any lower-tier money;
3. the jackpot is not shared;
4. all pre-existing carried value `B` is captured by us;

our gross cash cannot exceed:

`rS + B`.

Therefore a strict pre-tax profit requires:

`rS + B > S + C`

or

`B > (1-r)S + C`.

At the legal maximum `r = 0.80` and before any costs:

`B > 0.20 × 46,948,050`

`B > 9,389,610 AZN`.

This is a **necessary**, not sufficient, condition. Sharing, taxes, purchase limits and lower-tier leakage only increase the required external subsidy.

Sensitivity:
- r=80% -> required B > **9,389,610 AZN**;
- r=70% -> required B > **14,084,415 AZN**;
- r=60% -> required B > **18,779,220 AZN**;
- r=50% -> required B > **23,474,025 AZN**.

## Fixed-fund alternative
The Lottery Law also permits registered conditions to specify a fixed prize-fund amount instead of a sales-linked percentage.

If 4+4 uses a fixed-fund structure for some categories, a full-space purchase does **not** automatically cause prize funding to rise linearly with our 46.95m spend. That is economically worse for buy-the-pot unless the already-fixed/carryover pool itself exceeds the acquisition cost.

Therefore the unresolved registered conditions now matter primarily to distinguish:
- sales-linked current funding + carryover, where the >9.3896m external-subsidy hurdle applies;
- fixed funding, where the exact registered fixed fund must itself be compared with 46.948m spend.

Neither branch presently supports guaranteed profit.
