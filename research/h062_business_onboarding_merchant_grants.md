# H062 — regulated business-account onboarding / payroll / merchant-acquiring activation grants

Updated: 2026-08-17
Status: **MECHANISM CLASS REAL; CURRENT AZERBAIJAN PUBLIC SCREEN CLOSED; NO TERMINAL GUARANTEE**

## Goal
Search for a current, lawfully executable business-account / payroll / merchant-acquiring promotion that creates a **strictly positive cash floor** while preserving principal and avoiding lottery, first-N, invitation, discretionary or consumption-only mechanics.

The target is stronger than ordinary fee savings. A terminal SUCCESS needs a fixed positive amount that is vested before/through an independently owed business flow and survives all allowed branches after fees, reversals and withdrawal.

## Necessary-condition result
A pure fee waiver on a self-created transfer cannot by itself create positive absolute cash profit.

If initial principal is `P`, a qualifying self-transfer returns the same principal and the campaign merely changes a fee from `f>0` to `0`, then terminal cash under the promotion is at most `P` absent a separate cash grant/rebate. The waiver avoids a loss relative to the ordinary tariff; it does not make final cash exceed starting cash.

A fee waiver can still be economically valuable when the underlying business payment, payroll, tax payment, merchant receipt or transfer is **independently required**. In that case it creates incremental business savings. But it is not a self-starting guaranteed-profit strategy under the project's strict terminal definition unless a separate fixed cash reward is added.

## Current Azerbaijan screen

### Yelo Bank — `Welcome to Business` (ACTIVE)
Official Yelo announcement dated 2026-07-01 says the campaign was extended through **2026-09-30**. New first-time online business-account customers receive for 3 months, on turnover up to 100,000 AZN:
- 0% commission on domestic AZN transfers;
- 0% commission on AZN cash withdrawals;
- 0% commission on salary-project transfers (except All ATMs);
- a complimentary one-year business card;
- free online account opening / payroll card ordering / Mobile POS access.

Source: https://www.yelo.az/en/news/yelo-bank-extends-welcome-to-business-campaign/

This is deterministic and currently executable in Azerbaijan for eligible new business customers. However it is **fee avoidance / free service**, not withdrawable cash. It can improve margins on independently required transactions but has no positive cash floor for a user who opens an account solely to exploit the promotion.

Status: **VALID CURRENT FEE SUBSIDY; NOT TERMINAL GUARANTEE**.

### ABB Biz — `Lucky Individual Entrepreneur` (ACTIVE through 2026-08-18)
Official ABB campaign runs 2026-05-18 through 2026-08-18. Business activities (registration, >=100 AZN payments/transfers, debit-card orders, QR receipts, Mobile POS order, credit application, tax payment) generate chances in a prize lottery. Only ten entrepreneurs win prizes and winners are chosen randomly.

Source: https://abb-bank.az/en/kampaniyalar/sansli-ferdi-sahibkar-lotereyasi-sans-sizin-olsun

There is an allowed zero-prize branch for every participant. Therefore it fails the strict all-outcome positive-profit gate immediately.

Status: **REJECTED AS GUARANTEE**.

Source: https://www.accessbank.az/az/biznes/endirim50/

## What this packet closes
The current public Azerbaijan business-onboarding screen found three broad structures:
1. **deterministic fee waivers/discounts** — real and sometimes active, but no positive standalone cash floor;
2. **lottery/prize campaigns** — positive upside but explicit zero-prize branches;
3. **credit-linked cashback/discounts** — require debt/interest exposure and do not preserve principal.

No current public offer found in this packet simultaneously provides:
- fixed withdrawable cash;
- deterministic entitlement before commitment;
- Azerbaijan eligibility;
- self-controlled or independently owed qualification;
- preserved principal;
- no random/first-N/invite/discretion gate;
- no purchase/credit loss branch;
- bounded fees and no ordinary reversal/clawback branch.

## H062 terminal conclusion
**NO SUCCESS.** Business onboarding / merchant activation subsidies are economically real in Azerbaijan, and Yelo currently offers a substantial deterministic fee subsidy. But the current public screen contains no fixed-cash business grant that turns account activation or merchant/payroll use into strictly positive guaranteed net cash profit.

H062 should not be repeated without a new offer or new product-specific contract route.
