# H059 — marketplace / creator / freelancer payout bonuses

Updated: 2026-08-17
Status: **CURRENT PUBLIC SCREEN CLOSED; real bonus mechanisms found, no strict guaranteed-profit candidate**

## Goal
Search current public marketplace, creator and freelancer programs for the strongest possible version of the H058 idea: independently earned revenue or completed work mechanically triggers a fixed positive reward, ideally cash, without manufacturing circular spend.

A terminal candidate would need all of:
1. current public availability;
2. lawful executable eligibility for the target user/jurisdiction;
3. independently earned underlying revenue/work;
4. fixed positive cash reward;
5. deterministic qualification/vesting rather than invite, lottery, first-N or discretionary selection;
6. no dependence on an uncontrolled third party after commitment;
7. no pre-vesting cancellation/clawback branch that can reduce the floor to zero;
8. positive net after fees/tax/FX.

Candidate matrix: `data/derived/h059_marketplace_creator_bonus_screen.csv`.

## 1. AZDO — strongest local mechanism, but reward is not cash
Official Guaranteed Deal page currently states that users can receive **3% Umico bonuses** for Guaranteed Deal activity; the executor side is also described as receiving 3% Umico bonuses. The FAQ states that bonuses are an internal unit and **cannot be cashed out**; they can be spent on AZDO/service uses. Current referral FAQ also describes a 10-AZN bonus-account reward after a qualifying new user completes the required activity.

Sources:
- https://azdo.az/ru/zamanatli-sovdalasma
- https://azdo.az/ru/documents/faq

Why it matters: this is a real Azerbaijan marketplace where genuine completed work can trigger extra value. Why it is not terminal: the reward is service/partner credit rather than withdrawable cash, and referral reward additionally depends on an independent referred user.

## 2. Fiverr — a real fixed cash creator reward exists, but is not currently executable
Fiverr's current help material documents a **Freelancer Reward Program** under which eligible high-performing freelancers receive a **$10,000 cash bonus over four years, $2,500 annually**, subject to annual earnings/growth criteria. However:
- enrollment is currently closed;
- the program is open only to individuals defined as U.S. persons for tax purposes.

Source:
- https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/31990864544017-Fiverr-s-freelancer-equity-program

This validates the mechanism class strongly: marketplace revenue can indeed be paired with a fixed cash bonus. It does not provide a current executable guarantee for this project.

Fiverr's ordinary referral and freelancer-referral programs are weaker:
- standard referral reward = Fiverr Credits, not cash;
- freelancer referral reward = 2.5% of referred order value in Fiverr Credits;
- eligibility/program conditions are modifiable and the reward depends on another user's completed purchase/order.

Sources:
- https://www.fiverr.com/legal-portal/legal-terms/referral-program
- https://www.fiverr.com/legal-portal/legal-terms/seller-referral-terms

## 3. Upwork — no referral reward
Upwork's current official freelancer-referral help states there is **no incentive or reward** for referring another freelancer.

Source:
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405508078483-Understanding-freelancer-referrals-on-Upwork

Therefore this route has no subsidy to combine with independently earned platform revenue.

## 4. YouTube — fixed $200 creator bonus exists, but invite/geography/brand selection break determinism
YouTube's 2026 Affiliate Partnerships Boost Bonus offered **$200 for each eligible new Short** during the June 15–July 15 2026 campaign when that Short was boosted by an eligible brand/seller. Official conditions require:
- official email invitation;
- U.S. or South Korea location;
- the video must actually be selected/boosted by an eligible brand for at least one day.

Source:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17105253?hl=en

The reward is real cash via AdSense, but qualification contains both geography and an uncontrolled brand-selection branch, so there is no strict pre-commitment positive floor.

## 5. Adobe Stock — contribution bonus is non-cash
Adobe Stock's 2026 Contributor Bonus gives eligible active contributors a **12-month Creative Cloud Pro subscription**. Eligibility is based on active-contributor status, quality/acceptance rates and measurable value added to the collection.

Source:
- https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/royalties-pricing/bonus-program.html

This is useful value for a creator but not withdrawable fixed cash, and Adobe determines eligibility.

## 6. Freelancer.com — affiliate payment exists but still depends on third-party activity and program terms
Freelancer.com's Affiliate Program pays a Bonus based on the net project fees of projects posted by referred users, for the first 100 days of the referred account. The program also reserves modification/discontinuation rights and attribution/compliance conditions apply.

Source:
- https://www.freelancer.com/page.php?p=info%2Faffiliate_program

Mechanism is economically real but not a self-contained guaranteed-profit construction: an uncontrolled referred user's qualifying activity is required.

## Class-level result
H059 found multiple genuine marketplace/creator reward mechanisms, including two especially important controls:
- **fixed cash tied to creator earnings exists** (Fiverr Freelancer Reward Program);
- **genuine local work can trigger extra platform value** (AZDO 3% Umico bonus).

But the currently public candidates fail at least one terminal gate:
- non-cash reward;
- closed or geography-restricted enrollment;
- invitation/selection discretion;
- dependence on a referred buyer/freelancer/brand;
- program modification/cancellation rights.

Therefore no current H059 candidate proves a strictly positive guaranteed cash floor after all allowed branches.

## Next higher-value direction
Do not repeat broad marketplace/referral searches immediately. Move to **H060 deterministic transaction/funding credits from regulated cross-border payment providers**, specifically programs where an already-controlled transfer or funding action earns a fixed cash credit and where entitlement can vest before any market/event risk. This is structurally closer to a true guaranteed cash floor than creator bonuses dependent on third parties.
