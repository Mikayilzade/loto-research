## Concrete checkpoint

The current Reel Raffle `APP EXCLUSIVE! - Win £1000 Cash for FREE!` draw is a genuine zero-price finite-pool cash competition:

- £0.00 entry;
- £1,000 cash prize;
- 99,999 advertised maximum tickets;
- maximum 50 entries per person;
- 10,585 entries already present at the checked live snapshot;
- closes 27 September 2026.

For any single player, at most 50 of those 10,585 existing entries can be theirs. Therefore at least **10,535 existing entries are necessarily external**. A legal draw can select one of those external entries, yielding **£0 cash** to the player.

Thus the zero purchase price gives favorable EV but does **not** give a strictly positive worst-case cash floor. H310 is closed without needing assumptions about later sales or checkout mechanics.
