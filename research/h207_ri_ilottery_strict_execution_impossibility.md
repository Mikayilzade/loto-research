# H207 — Rhode Island iLottery strict-execution impossibility

Updated: 2026-08-23
Scope: LOTTERY ONLY
Status: **ONLINE/iLOTTERY STRICT-GUARANTEE BRANCH CLOSED; LOTTERY RESEARCH NOT TERMINAL**

## Target
Continue the H173/H175 Rhode Island Keno execution branch without repeating H206's unresolved search for numerical Purchase Limits. Test a more fundamental question:

> Even if the VIP Plus purchase maxima, Keno cart capacity and draw-break duration were recovered and were all large enough, can the current Rhode Island iLottery channel itself support a **strictly guaranteed** acquisition of every required same-draw Keno wager?

This matters because terminal project SUCCESS requires guaranteed positive net profit under every allowed execution branch, not merely a high-probability operational plan.

## Current primary-source evidence

### 1. Current iLottery Terms expressly deny timely-purchase assurance
Rhode Island Lottery's current Registration / Terms page states in the Purchases section that purchases are subject to account/Lottery limits and that the Rhode Island Lottery:

- **does not guarantee the timeliness of any purchase**;
- may **delay a purchase** until verified to its satisfaction in its sole discretion;
- may **refuse any attempted purchase for any reason**;
- is not liable for loss caused by delay or denial.

Source: https://www.rilot.com/en-us/registration.html

This is not merely a theoretical system-failure caveat. It creates an expressly permitted execution branch in which one or more required Keno wagers are not accepted for the target draw.

### 2. The same Terms reserve broader service/account discretion
The current Terms also reserve the Lottery's right, in its sole discretion, to terminate/suspend/cancel an iLottery account and to modify, interrupt, limit, suspend or discontinue services/products without notice. Geolocation is likewise not guaranteed to verify every physically present player; failure of geolocation prohibits purchase.

Source: https://www.rilot.com/en-us/registration.html

### 3. Current FAQ confirms Keno cart submissions can be voided / unavailable during allowed windows
The current FAQ says Keno is among draw games purchased through cart submissions. It also states that submitted cart purchases during scheduled maintenance are voided and that iLottery wagering is unavailable during each game's draw-break period.

Source: https://www.rilot.com/en-us/player-zone/faqs.html

### 4. Current 2026 Keno rules preserve the mathematical execution target
Official 2026 Rhode Island Lottery rules still define Keno draws every four minutes and Registered Ticketless Play as an accepted iLottery wager recorded in the gaming system. Therefore a wager that is delayed, refused, voided or not recorded for the intended draw is not part of the required same-draw cover.

Source: https://www.rilot.com/content/dam/interactive/ilottery/pdfs/about-us/RILotteryRules2026.pdf

## Exact implication for H173/H175
H173 and H175 require complete ownership of a prescribed collection of distinct same-draw 3-spot wagers before the target draw:

- H175: 4,336 paid selections in the candidate hybrid;
- H173: 4,560 paid selections in the proven doubled cover.

A strict guarantee therefore needs a rule or contract forcing acceptance of **every required wager for the intended draw** once the player satisfies stated conditions.

Current iLottery Terms establish the opposite: refusal/delay is an allowed operator action. Thus the outcome branch

`at least one required cover wager is refused/delayed past target draw`

is legally/contractually admissible.

In that branch the designed all-outcome payoff theorem no longer applies because the purchased portfolio is incomplete. Therefore iLottery cannot be the sole execution channel for a terminal guaranteed-profit proof under current terms.

This conclusion is independent of:
- the numerical daily/weekly/monthly Purchase Limits;
- the number of Keno selections per cart;
- cart monetary capacity;
- the exact draw-break duration;
- raw interface speed.

Those values still matter for practical/high-probability execution and positive-EV work, but recovering favorable values cannot by itself cure the strict-guarantee defect.
