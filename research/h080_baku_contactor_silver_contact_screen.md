- LC1D09 through LC1D18 power contacts: **90% silver / 10% nickel**;

Source: https://www.se.com/us/en/faqs/FA109028/

Therefore the exact advertised LC1D09 and LC1D32 models are not merely generic e-scrap: their power-contact alloy is officially known to be silver-rich.
