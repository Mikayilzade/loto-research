## Net-A-Deal — £10,000 Instant Wins August / August #2 | Every Ticket Wins

Checked: 2026-08-27
Outcome: **CLOSED — zero strict withdrawable-cash floor**

What was tested:
- current live 49,999-ticket finite pool;
- 1,000-entry per-person cap;
- £0.49 paid entry;
- 32 cash instant-win identifiers totaling £9,000;
- 40 £25 Site Credit identifiers totaling £1,000 face value;
- 49,927 residual identifiers that only enter a separate £5,000 rewards draw;
- random ticket-number allocation.

Why closed:
`49,927` residual identifiers exceed the `1,000` per-person maximum. A legal random allocation can therefore put all allowed entries in the residual class, and the separate rewards draw can legally be lost. Guaranteed withdrawable cash = `£0`.

Reopen trigger:
Only material rule changes that give every residual identifier deterministic withdrawable value, or controllable/reservable ticket selection that eliminates the zero-cash allocation.
