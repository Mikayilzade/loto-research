## Rule added by H096
For reimbursement-only schemes with support rate `r <= 1` applied to the same qualifying cost `C`, the direct subsidy loop has floor `-(1-r)C <= 0`. Such schemes cannot be standalone guaranteed-profit constructions unless a separate legally locked transfer/value stream exceeds all unreimbursed and execution costs.
