## H307 — Salexmo visible instant-win identifiers

Status: **CLOSED / TARGETING-BLOCKED**.

Candidate checked: `£300 CASH FOR 3P, PLUS CASH INSTANT WINS 11/9/26`.

Why it looked promising:
- finite 40,000-entry pool;
- £0.03 paid tickets;
- ten £30 instant-win identifiers publicly listed, nine shown `Available!` at the checked snapshot;
- page language says `Choose Your Tickets!`.

Why it fails rigorously:
- governing Terms §4.1(B) say entry numbers are **randomly allocated on completion/payment**;
- free-route entrants use the same allocation method;
- ticket numbers are disclosed after order confirmation;
- 34,408 non-instant identifiers remained versus a 10,000 per-user cap, so a legal allocation exists in which every allowed player entry misses every instant win;
- the main draw also has legal external winners because the player cannot own the complete pool and 5,583 entries were already sold.

Strict withdrawable-cash floor: **£0**.

Reusable rule: do not treat a published `Available!` instant-win number as targetable unless the exact numerical identifier can be selected and reserved before payment under binding rules.
