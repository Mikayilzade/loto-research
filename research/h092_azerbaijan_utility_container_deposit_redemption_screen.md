# H092 — Azerbaijan utility / returnable-container mandatory-redemption screen

Updated: 2026-08-19
Status: **SCREENED / NO EXECUTABLE GUARANTEED-CASH ARBITRAGE FOUND**

## Goal
Continue the deterministic-cash search after H091 by screening local Azerbaijan instruments that can look like refundable deposits or returnable assets:
- electricity/water customer accounts;
- LPG cylinders;
- pallets / reusable crates and similar transport packaging.

The target is not ordinary resale margin. A terminal candidate must have a legally/contractually fixed cash redemption or buyback right, lawful transfer/acquisition, a pre-purchase eligibility lock, and `redemption cash > all-in acquisition + execution cost`.

## 1. Electricity customer accounts — account/property-linked, not a transferable cash instrument
Current Azərişıq/ASAN procedures show that changing the named household consumer requires identity, subscriber code and property/right-of-use evidence (or a notarized transfer route in limited cases). Debt must be cleared before the change is performed.

This is an account/service relationship tied to an address/property entitlement, not a bearer balance instrument with a fixed cash redemption floor.

Sources:
- https://asan.gov.az/service/asan-kommunal/azerisiq-asc-terefinden-goesterilen-xidmetler/ehali-istehlakcisinin-ad-deyisdirilmesi
- https://azerishiq.az/menu/customer-service

Conclusion: **no transferable deposit-like cash arbitrage identified**.

## 2. Water customer accounts — likewise tied to property/use rights
ADSEA states that when the owner/user changes, the old consumer settles debt and the new consumer enters a new agreement. The name-change process requires identity, subscriber code and ownership/right-of-use documents.

Again, this is a service-account transition rather than a bearer/refundable instrument that can be bought below a legally fixed cash redemption value.

Sources:
- https://adsea.gov.az/elaqe/tez-tez-verilen-suallar
- https://asan.gov.az/service/asan-kommunal/azerbaycan-doevlet-su-ehtiyatlari-agentliyi-terefinden-goesterilen-xidmetler/abonent-melumatlarinda-deyisiklik-edilmesi

Conclusion: **no transferable deposit-like cash arbitrage identified**.

## 3. LPG cylinders — exchange mechanism exists, fixed cash redemption not found
A current Baku operator advertises immediate exchange of an empty LPG cylinder for a full one. Azerbaijan LPG-safety rules also expressly contemplate reception, storage and dispatch of cylinders at filling facilities.

However the evidence found supports a physical exchange/refill market, not a mandatory issuer cash-refund amount for any lawful holder of a cylinder. Current Baku classifieds also price empty/used household LPG cylinders as ordinary goods (for example roughly 45–50 AZN in indexed listings), which reinforces commodity/exchange treatment rather than bearer-deposit treatment.

Sources:
- https://lalafo.az/baku/ads/msm-company-bos-qaz-balonlarinin-dolusu-il-dyisdirilmsi-id-111860723
- https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/5/c_f_5387.html
- current indexed Lalafo Baku LPG-cylinder listings.

Conclusion: **exchange value validated; mandatory fixed cash-redemption floor not found**.

## 4. Pallets / reusable crates — secondary commodity market, no mandatory issuer redemption
Azerbaijan has an active packaging/pallet market. Tara.az currently lists examples such as:
- standard 80×120 wooden pallet: 8.50 AZN;
- EPAL 80×120 second sort: 9 AZN;
- EPAL 80×120 first sort: 11 AZN;
- small plastic crate/tara products at sub-AZN levels.

Other indexed local classified-market pages show pallet/crate buying and selling. But no operator/statutory rule was found that obliges an issuer/pool to redeem any qualifying pallet/crate presented by a bearer for a fixed cash amount.

This is therefore ordinary resale/recycling, already covered by the transaction-level bid-before-buy framework (H076/H078/H079), not a new deterministic redemption class.

Sources:
- https://tara.az/
- https://unvan.az/palet-alisi-satisi
- https://tezbazar.az/plastik%2Byesik%2Balis%2Bsatisi

Conclusion: **secondary market exists; no fixed mandatory cash redemption**.

## Class-level result
For the Azerbaijan examples screened here, the apparent `deposit / returnable asset` intuition collapses into one of two structures:
1. **account/property-linked service rights** (electricity/water), where the balance/account is not a freely transferable bearer claim; or
2. **ordinary reusable physical assets** (LPG cylinders, pallets/crates), where exchange/resale value exists but no fixed mandatory cash redemption floor was found.

Neither structure currently supplies a guaranteed positive cash floor from lawful below-redemption acquisition.

## Operational rule
Do not repeat generic Azerbaijan utility/pallet/cylinder searches unless new evidence contains at least one of:
- an explicit fixed cash refund/buyback amount;
- a rule that any lawful holder/transferee may claim it;
- a live acquisition ask below that amount;
- a pre-payment validation mechanism for eligibility/status.

## Next research direction
Continue systematic class expansion into instruments where redemption is more naturally bearer-like and price is externally fixed: transferable coupons/vouchers with mandatory cash-out, statutory recycling/bounty certificates, refundable commercial container deposits with explicit bearer redemption, or regulated security deposits whose refund claim can lawfully transfer independently of the underlying service contract.
