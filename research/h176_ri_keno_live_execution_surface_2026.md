### What this changes
H173/H175 previously treated online Keno only as a possible execution channel. H176 confirms that direct online number selection and purchase is indeed a current official channel, and that online tickets are non-cancellable.

However, the public form visibly represents a **single number-selection workflow** plus consecutive-game repetition. It does not publicly expose:
- a CSV/bulk combination upload;
- an API for submitting thousands of distinct selections;
- an atomic basket guarantee across thousands of Keno selections;
- a documented facility to pre-stage 4,336/4,560 distinct selections for one target draw;
- a public guarantee that a qualifying retail-only promotion also applies to iKeno/online purchases.

The absence of those controls on the public page is not a proof that no internal or retailer bulk method exists; it means the execution gate remains unproven.
