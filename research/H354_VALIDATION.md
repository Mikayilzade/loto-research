# H354 VALIDATION — Spanish Lotería Nacional unique-series cover

Date: 2026-08-29

## Independent checks

1. Official 2026 Thursday draw dossier: **6 series × 100,000 billetes × €30 = €18,000,000 issue**.
2. The same dossier states **€12,600,000** total prizes = exactly **70%** of issue.
3. Each billete is divided into **10 décimos of €3**. A series is one billete of every participating number; therefore one fixed fraction across all 100,000 numbers of one series is exactly 100,000 unique décimos.
4. Acquisition cost: `100,000 × €3 = €300,000`.
5. Prize allocation is repeated per series. One series receives `€12,600,000 / 6 = €2,100,000` at billete scale; one décimo fraction receives one tenth = **€210,000**.
6. Exact net: `€210,000 - €300,000 = -€90,000`; return = **70%**.
7. Stronger impossible-perfect entire-issue takeover is also exactly `€12.6m / €18m = 70%`; no partial identifier-ownership issue can improve the guaranteed aggregate ratio without an external subsidy/discount.

Arithmetic inconclusive: **0**.
Closure-relevant inconclusive: **0**.

## Structural conclusion

This candidate satisfies the structural target that defeated many earlier packets: identifiers are finite by series/fraction and prizes are not diluted by unrelated external copies of the same number. Nevertheless the guaranteed economics are intrinsically below break-even because the complete issued prize pool is fixed at 70% of issue value.
