# Next Search Playbook

Updated: 2026-08-29
Mission: find a strict guaranteed positive net-cash construction without spending real money or creating packets for obvious failures.

## Gate 0 — novelty and evidence
Before analysis, search `data/derived/h_packet_inventory.json`, `research/H_PACKET_INDEX.md` and `research/MECHANISM_FILTERS.md`. State the materially new rule, promotion, channel or control condition. If none exists, stop: a renamed game or larger headline jackpot is not a new hypothesis.

Preserve official URL, retrieval date, rule-version/effective date, jurisdiction and an immutable snapshot/hash when permitted. Separate current contractual fact from page copy and assumptions.

## Mandatory decision tree

### 1. Name the external value/control mechanism
Choose exactly one primary thesis:
- external subsidy/overlay not funded solely by our own purchases;
- every-ID positive withdrawable cash;
- finite exclusive identifiers;
- binding discount/free allocation;
- principal plus mandatory positive increment.

If the proposal is only ordinary payout funded from sub-100% ticket sales, apply F1 and reject cheaply.

### 2. Does any legal zero-withdrawable-cash branch remain?
Build the smallest outcome-class table. Count losing IDs, non-cash/site-credit prizes, external single-winner states, void/non-acceptance branches and principal-only states. If any positive-cost attempt can return zero cash, reject under F5/F7 unless exhaustive control removes that branch.

### 3. Can external duplicates dilute the guarantee?
Identify whether each key payout is fixed per winning entry or divided/capped. For a pool, solve the integer duplicate break-even threshold. If the permitted external count is unbounded or reaches the threshold, reject under F2. Probability that duplicates are rare is irrelevant to a guarantee.

### 4. Does our portfolio prevent the special branch?
Write the promotion/rolldown trigger literally. If full coverage ensures the top-tier winner whose absence is required, reject under F3. Never add both own top-prize receipt and a mutually exclusive no-winner rolldown.

### 5. Is acquisition binding, atomic and exclusive enough?
Trace the process from first irreversible cost to accepted identifier ownership. Check per-player cap, already-sold inventory, public sales after reservation, postal loss, operator refusal/voiding, cancellation and withdrawal rules. Reject under F4/F6/F9 if a legal execution branch loses cost or control.

### 6. Compute a fast necessary break-even bound
Use deliberately favourable arithmetic:

`upper-bound guaranteed gross = fixed controlled cash + maximum non-dilutable external subsidy + redeemable principal`

`all-in lower-bound cost = price/fees/postage/tax/required add-ons + unavoidable execution cost`

Require strict `gross > cost`. Also compute subsidy hurdle:

`required subsidy > all-in cost - base guaranteed cash`.

For a shareable pool, include the worst legally permitted duplicate count, not zero duplicates. For a finite issue, first compare total guaranteed cash to the cheapest complete-acquisition cost. If the favourable bound is <=100%, reject under F1/F8.

### 7. Only then run exact work
Only a candidate passing Gates 1-6 may receive:
- exact combinatorial/category partition;
- optimizer/cover design;
- independent implementation or invariant checks;
- source-normalized derived JSON;
- a numbered H packet with report, validation, status and ledger append.

The exact model must state every legal outcome, baseline, costs, uncertainty and reopening condition. Assign H358 only for a substantive survivor, never for documenting a cheap filter rejection.

## Priority lanes

### Lane A — all-ID cash with binding digital acquisition (highest priority)
Required evidence:
- complete finite ID/prize vector;
- strictly positive **withdrawable cash** on every reachable ID;
- zero/low-cost digital issuance expressly granted in governing terms;
- binding allocation before irreversible cost;
- per-person entitlement/control sufficient for the proposed floor.

Fast metric: `minimum cash per binding allocation - all-in marginal cost`. Full takeover is unnecessary if every individual allocated ID has a positive strict net floor.

### Lane B — external full-cover subsidy without dilution
Required evidence:
- isolated exact/full-cover lower bound above 100%;
- subsidy paid even when our portfolio hits top tier;
- fixed per winning entry or hard duplicate cap below exact break-even;
- feasible sales capacity, cutoff, tax and claim structure.

Search terms/mechanisms: guaranteed add-on per match, promotional fixed tier, overlay allocated per winning selection, deterministic multipliers free of surcharge. Reject shareable headline jackpots early.

### Lane C — actually monopolizable finite identifiers
Required evidence:
- hard certified universe and remaining inventory;
- player-selectable identifiers or atomic whole-pool reservation;
- no already-held/external eligible winners after reservation;
- guaranteed cash liability above full acquisition and execution cost;
- full ownership itself forces any reserve/jackpot payment.

A finite universe alone does not pass: H262/H267 show that open issuance or a separate non-forced jackpot draw defeats control.

### Lane D — principal plus binding positive minimum (lower priority)
Require redeemable principal **and** a contractual positive per-holder/per-entry cash increment. Random prizes and higher expected fund rates fail. Include lock-up, inflation, tax, redemption delay and opportunity cost after the nominal minimum first passes.

## Explicit depriorities
Do not spend deep-work time on:
- ordinary jackpot/full-cover systems with unbounded sharing;
- generic Keno, Pick-3 or standard fixed prize tables already dominated by F1;
- site-credit/free-play `Every Ticket Wins` without unconditional cash withdrawal;
- postal free-entry candidates unless new binding acceptance defeats F6;
- paid multipliers that scale price and payout equally;
- no-winner rolldowns blocked by our own cover;
- a closed H packet without a documented material rule/evidence change.

## Cheap-screen record template (no H number)
Record in the session search log:
1. candidate, jurisdiction, official URL and retrieval/rule date;
2. lane and claimed mechanism;
3. first failed filter;
4. decisive rule/fact and one-line bound;
5. precise condition that would justify reconsideration.

This provides coverage without inflating the H namespace.

## Escalation checklist
A new packet is justified only when every answer is **YES**:
- materially new versus inventory;
- cash, not restricted value;
- no legal zero-cash execution branch;
- no uncontrolled dilution;
- portfolio does not destroy trigger;
- binding and sufficient control;
- favourable necessary bound strictly above cost;
- exact work could change the decision rather than merely restate the cheap screen.
