## Stronger legal acceptance reading for incomplete vehicles
Primary law says:
1. vehicle acceptance by registered utilizers is **free of charge**;
2. before utilization the vehicle must be permanently deregistered;
3. the utilizer requires the utilization application, disposal authority if needed, written deregistration information, and identity/legal-person document;
4. when those documents contain no deficiency/inconsistency, the acceptance act is prepared;
5. the act must record the vehicle's technical condition/completeness and **missing parts**;
6. after acceptance, the confirmation document is issued within two working days.

Primary source:
- https://president.az/az/articles/view/60617

This is stronger than the earlier generic inference. The statutory workflow expressly anticipates missing parts and ties the act step to document sufficiency. Therefore **incompleteness alone is not evidenced as a categorical rejection ground**. This does not prove that the specific 950-AZN vehicle has intact identity markers or satisfies all deregistration/document requirements.
