Updated: 2026-08-12
Status: **strong lead; not yet a pure category-II payout observation**

## Why category II matters
Category II is the grouped 4+3 / 3+4 state. Exact probability per base variant is:

`0.000005452835634281` = about **1 in 183,390.82**.

In the currently preserved 2026 secondary payout tables, category II repeatedly has zero winners and its assigned pre-draw pool is not displayed. That makes category II one of the most important missing components in both ordinary EV and zero-winner carryover research.

## Interpretation
For a single ordinary 4+4 variant, being one number short of 4+4 means exactly 7 of the 8 required numbers matched, which corresponds to category II (4+3 or 3+4).

Therefore this is strong primary evidence that Nizami's ticket contained at least one category-II winning variant.

However, **do not yet set category-II per-winner prize = 8,609 AZN**. The current game allows 5+5 / 6+6 combination tickets and a ticket can win across multiple categories/variants. The reported 8,609 AZN may therefore be the ticket's aggregate payout rather than the pure category-II amount.
