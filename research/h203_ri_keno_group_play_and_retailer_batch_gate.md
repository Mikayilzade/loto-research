## Target
Test the remaining H201/H202 operational shortcut: whether Rhode Island Lottery provides an official group-play or retailer batch infrastructure that could make acquisition of all 4,336/4,560 specified same-draw Keno selections an executable guaranteed step.

## Fresh official evidence

### 1. Official Group Play is not a Keno purchasing mechanism
The current Rhode Island Lottery Group Play page explicitly frames its tools around players pooling money for **Powerball and Mega Millions**. The page says the tools organize the group, track players/payments and assist prize claiming; it does not present Keno as an eligible Group Play game and does not provide an atomic group-purchase facility.

This closes the inference that the site's visible `Group Play` feature supplies an official Keno syndicate execution channel.

Official source: https://www.rilot.com/en-us/group-play.html

### 2. Current Retailer Point-of-Sale material does not expose Keno batch mechanics
The current Retailer's Corner Point of Sale Material page publishes printable material for Mega Millions, Powerball, Wild Money and combination signs. It does **not** expose a Keno play-slip/batch/QR-capacity manual or retailer-terminal instruction that proves one scan can encode hundreds/thousands of independently specified same-draw Keno selections.

This does not prove the terminal lacks such functionality; it proves the public official POS route does not establish it.

Official source: https://www.rilot.com/en-us/retailers-corner/retailer-point.html

### 3. Historical Keno QR evidence is ticket-identification, not batch-entry evidence
Official 2025 Keno Changeup Second Chance rules state that eligible Keno tickets carry a 23-character Second Chance Code and a QR code that can be scanned in the mobile app. Entries scale with the **dollar value of the Keno ticket**. This confirms Keno tickets can represent purchases larger than $1 and carry QR identifiers, but the documented QR is for second-chance ticket submission after purchase. It does not establish pre-purchase encoding of multiple arbitrary Keno combinations or an atomic same-draw bulk order.

Official source: https://www.rilot.com/content/dam/interactive/ilottery/pdfs/Promotions/2025/KenoChangeUpRules-July2025.pdf
