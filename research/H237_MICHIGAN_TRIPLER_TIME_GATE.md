# H237 — Michigan Tripler Time all-coverage gate

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: NOT SUCCESS

## Question

Does Michigan's current August 2026 Club Keno Tripler Time promotion provide the missing executable mechanism from H236: a pre-draw, controllable 2X-or-better status for every 3-spot wager in the required coverage?

## Current official evidence

Michigan Lottery's 2026-07-30 promotion announcement states that the August promotion begins August 1 and that Club Keno tickets purchased at retailers statewide are eligible at no additional cost. It states that **if** a player receives a Doubler or Tripler message on the ticket, eligible prizes are doubled or tripled. A multi-draw ticket that receives such a message gets the multiplier across all eligible winnings for all drawings on that ticket.

The Michigan Lottery's published FY2026 plan independently describes August 2026 as one of three one-month Club Keno bonus-payout promotions.

Historical Michigan Lottery Game Line material makes the assignment mechanism explicit: **random Club Keno tickets** print with a Doubler or Tripler message. This is consistent with the conditional wording in the current 2026 announcement.

## Consequence

The promotion is a genuine pre-draw ticket-level overlay, which is stronger than Rhode Island's post/random multiplier draw in H177. However, the player cannot choose or force the Doubler/Tripler tag on an arbitrary selected ticket.

Therefore H236's missing premise remains unproved: there is no evidence that all 82,160 distinct 3-spot combinations can be acquired with guaranteed 2X-or-better status for the same target draw.

A multi-draw tagged ticket is useful because the tag persists across its drawings, but that does not eliminate random tag acquisition. Repeated purchase can raise probability of obtaining a tagged copy of a desired wager, but any finite sequence still has a nonzero failure probability unless the promotion rules impose a deterministic Nth-ticket award or another bounded guarantee. No such guarantee was found for Michigan Tripler Time.

## Verdict

NOT SUCCESS.

Michigan Tripler Time is confirmed as a live, free, pre-draw random overlay, but it is not a controllable all-ticket doubler. The exact doubled-coverage profitability result from H236 remains conditional rather than a guaranteed executable strategy.

## Next action

H238: formalize the random-tag acquisition problem. Derive the probability/cost/time bounds for obtaining tagged copies of every required 3-spot wager under hypothetical tag probabilities, and prove whether any finite purchase protocol can provide a strict 100% guarantee. Separately continue searching for promotions with deterministic Nth-ticket or all-ticket multipliers.
