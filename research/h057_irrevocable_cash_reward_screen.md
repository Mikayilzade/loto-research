# H057 — irrevocably vested cash reward screen

Updated: 2026-08-17
Status: **NO SUCCESS; current fixed-cash mechanisms exist, but no Azerbaijan-executable self-controlled reward survives all terminal gates**

## Goal
Find a current, lawful program accessible from Azerbaijan where a user can complete deterministic self-controlled actions, retain/recover principal, and receive a fixed positive cash reward that cannot be lost under allowed cancellation, clawback, market-value, third-party, invitation, or insolvency branches.

This packet deliberately excludes ordinary expected-value cashback from consumption unless the qualifying principal itself is recoverable in cash.

## Current screen

### 1. Payoneer Prestige Club 2026
Official current page confirms real cashback through 31 December 2026. Qualifying activity can include Payoneer card spend, supplier payments, bank withdrawals and out-of-network payments.

However:
- participation is only for **invited customers**;
- each participant receives privately communicated targets;
- products/eligibility vary by jurisdiction;
- exceeding a target earns a capped reward, but arbitrary-user public Azerbaijan eligibility plus a public deterministic target/reward is not established.

Result: **real cash mechanism, not a universal executable guarantee**.

Source: https://pages.payoneer.com/prestigeclub/

## Necessary-condition filter established by H057
A fixed-reward promotion is terminally interesting only if all are true before capital commitment:
1. Azerbaijan eligibility is public and current;
2. no invitation / first-N / discretionary-selection dependency;
3. qualifying actions are fully controlled by the user;
4. qualifying principal can be recovered in cash without relying on resale/consumption value;
5. reward is fixed cash or has a contractual positive cash floor;
6. vesting is deterministic and not market-price dependent;
7. cancellation, reversal, insolvency, abuse-review and program-change branches cannot remove both reward and principal;
8. all fees, tax, FX and withdrawal costs leave strictly positive net cash.

None of the current candidates in this packet satisfy all eight simultaneously.

## New research direction
The highest-value remaining H057 sub-class is **business payment-volume rebates where the underlying payment is independently owed anyway**. Economically, such rebates can create true incremental cash without consuming principal, but terminal proof requires a real pre-existing business payment obligation plus public Azerbaijan eligibility and a non-discretionary reward schedule. Payoneer demonstrates that the mechanism exists, but current public campaigns remain invite/target gated.

## Conclusion
**H057 did not produce SUCCESS.** It substantially narrows the current reward landscape:
- fixed cash: real but generally invite/target/spend/third-party dependent;
- self-funded broker bonuses: currently stock/variable value;
- local balance yield: positive but accrued over time, so not strict all-branch guarantee;
- local cashback: useful overlay but normally consumes principal through purchases.

Next highest-value search: publicly scheduled Azerbaijan-compatible **business-volume/payment-rail rebates** and self-controlled transfer/funding rewards with fixed cash, followed by genuinely new H052 contract routes.
