## H099 class result
A genuine mandatory state-payment rule exists, but the public procedure does not currently provide a transferable, pre-acquisition locked entitlement that an ordinary buyer can purchase below face value with a strictly positive worst-case floor.

Next search: transferable already-valued public claims/certificates and fixed buyback/surrender programs where payout survives holder transfer and validity can be confirmed before irreversible payment.
