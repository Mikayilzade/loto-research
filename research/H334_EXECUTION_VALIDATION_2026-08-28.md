## Physical marginal-cost check
Royal Mail currently publishes Second Class standard-letter postage at **£0.91**. A5 is 210 x 148 mm, within Royal Mail's standard letter maximum 240 x 165 mm, provided the item also meets thickness/weight limits.

The prior 9p non-postage hurdle is feasible on material alone. Spingold publishes 5,000 A5 black single-sided postcards for £180 before VAT, equivalent to 3.6p each before VAT or 4.32p each after applying 20% VAT. This gives an indicative postage + card direct cost of **£0.9532**, leaving **£0.0468** against the minimum £1 instant-cash outcome conditional on acceptance. This does not price the entrant's own manual labour.

Audrey Terms reject entries automatically generated by computer. The material benchmark therefore does not assume automated entrant data: pre-cut/preprinted blank stock can be hand-completed. This resolves the narrow material-cost question but not acceptance.

Audrey wallet Terms set minimum cash withdrawal at £5. Five accepted minimum-tier £1 wins would therefore be sufficient to cross the withdrawal threshold.

## Acceptance/transport check
The free route requires each entry to be separately posted and received before the Closing Date/cap. Current terms/page also establish:
- proof of postage does not guarantee entry;
- Promoter accepts no responsibility for lost or delayed entries;
- hand delivered entries are rejected;
- recorded delivery entries are rejected;
- entries that arrive after close/cap do not enter;
- incomplete/illegible/non-compliant entries can be disqualified.

Therefore there exists a permitted execution state in which a perfectly prepared postcard is posted, its postage/card cost is irreversibly incurred, but it is delayed/lost/not received before the applicable cutoff and no competition identifier is issued. That branch has negative net cash, so a strict everywhere-positive profit cannot be certified.
