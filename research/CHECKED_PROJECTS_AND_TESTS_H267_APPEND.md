# CHECKED PROJECTS AND TESTS — H267 APPEND

## H267 — Australian Lucky Lotteries Super/Mega Jackpot finite-pool takeover

**Mechanism tested:** complete ownership of a hard finite unique identifier pool combined with an accumulated external jackpot reserve.

**Why tested:** Lucky Lotteries have several unusually favorable structural properties: finite sequential identifiers, duplicate cancellation, draw only after sellout, guaranteed fixed prizes, and a jackpot that can grow from prior draws.

**Strong test granted:** impossible-perfect ownership of every identifier in the current draw, with all free-ticket consolation awards valued at their cash-equivalent subscription amount.

**Exact results:**
- Super Jackpot: 270,000-entry retail buyout A$594,000; legal no-jackpot guaranteed gross A$194,870; **32.8063973%** return.
- Mega Jackpot: 200,000-entry retail buyout A$1,100,000; legal no-jackpot guaranteed gross A$361,425; **32.8568182%** return.
- Even removing all commission gives only 36.0870% and 36.1425%, matching the products' published Prize Fund percentages.

**Decisive blocker:** owning every ticket identifier does not force the accumulated Jackpot Prize to be paid. The separately drawn Jackpot Number must also equal one of the cash-prize Winning Numbers; a legal non-coincidence branch exists, on which the jackpot rolls forward.

**Disposition:** **CLOSED / REJECTED** for strict guaranteed-profit takeover. Reopen only if complete ownership is made sufficient to force the accumulated jackpot, or a deterministic external subsidy becomes independent of the jackpot-trigger coincidence.

Primary rules checked: Tattersall's Rules of Authorised Lotteries, Schedule F, effective for draws/entries on and after 18 May 2025.
