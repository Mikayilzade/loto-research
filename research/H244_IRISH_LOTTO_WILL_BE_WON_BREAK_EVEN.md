# H244 — Irish Lotto Will-Be-Won / cap-draw break-even gate

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: CONDITIONAL POSITIVE STRUCTURE, NOT A GUARANTEE / NOT CURRENTLY ACTIONABLE

## Why this was tested

H243 rejected ordinary Lotto Plus optimization and redirected the search toward externally funded carryover, roll-down and must-be-won events. The current Irish Lotto 6/47 rules provide exactly such a mechanism.

## Official rule facts

Current Issue 9 Lotto 6/47 rules state:

- one Play costs EUR2, with a minimum two Plays / EUR4 ticket;
- the Prize Fund is expected to equal 52% of Gross Revenue;
- 15.80% of the Prize Fund is assigned to a Reserve Prize Fund, so the ordinary prize-category pools listed for the draw sum to 84.20% of the Prize Fund;
- the current jackpot cap is EUR18.96m;
- excess jackpot allocation at the cap flows down to the next higher prize tier with a winner;
- no more than five successive draws are allowed at the jackpot cap, and on the fifth cap draw an unwon jackpot rolls down to the next higher winning tier;
- a separately designated Will Be Won Draw also rolls an unwon jackpot down to the next higher winning tier.

## Full-space structural bound

For 6/47 there are C(47,6) = 10,737,573 distinct lines.

At EUR2 per line, buying every combination once costs:

EUR21,475,146.

The listed current-draw prize-category pools represent approximately:

52% * 84.20% = 43.784% of current Gross Revenue.

If one player were able to buy the whole combination space, if the stated expected allocation held exactly, and if no external players diluted pari-mutuel prizes, the current-draw component generated by that stake would therefore be approximately:

EUR21,475,146 * 43.784% = EUR9,402,677.92.

The external carryover needed merely to reach break-even under that conservative prize-pool accounting is therefore:

EUR21,475,146 - EUR9,402,677.92 = EUR12,072,468.08.

That threshold is materially below the current EUR18.96m jackpot cap.

At a full EUR18.96m carried jackpot, the same idealized accounting gives:

EUR18,960,000 + EUR9,402,677.92 - EUR21,475,146 = +EUR6,887,531.92

before operational friction.

## Important caveat: this is NOT a guaranteed-profit construction

The calculation above establishes a real structural positive-payout region, not an executable guarantee.

The fatal uncertainties are:

1. Pari-mutuel dilution: other players can share the jackpot and every variable prize pool.
2. Sales feedback: a EUR21.5m purchase itself massively changes Gross Revenue and pool sizes.
3. Rule 3 permits the Company to restrict/prohibit participation it considers to interfere with other players' reasonable access.
4. Transaction capacity, retailer/system limits and cutoff timing may make full-space purchase impossible.
5. Reserve-fund deployment is discretionary; it cannot be counted as guaranteed current-draw payout.
6. The 6/47 game is being replaced by 6/45 in September 2026, so this exact state space and these exact rules are about to expire.

## Current actionability check

The latest published draw checked (22 Aug 2026) showed a jackpot of only EUR2,669,369, far below the ~EUR12.07m idealized full-coverage break-even carryover and far below the EUR18.96m cap.

Because the 6/45 change launches in early September, the old 6/47 cap state is not a realistic live execution candidate unless an exceptional designated Will Be Won event is announced before the transition.

## Result

This is the first strong confirmation in the current search branch that carryover can mathematically overwhelm the house take: the carried jackpot is external to the current draw's stake and can push aggregate payout above aggregate purchase cost.

But it does NOT yet meet the project's success criterion because dilution and execution constraints destroy a guaranteed lower bound.
