# H095 — Azerbaijan Energy Efficiency Fund grants/subsidies

Updated: 2026-08-19
Status: **VALIDATED CURRENT SUBSIDY CLASS / NOT STANDALONE GUARANTEED PROFIT**

## Hypothesis
A current state energy-efficiency grant/subsidy could create a deterministic cash inflow large enough to exceed the applicant's locked project cost, producing a strictly positive worst-case cash floor.

## Current legal mechanism
Azerbaijan's Energy Efficiency Fund was created by Presidential Decree No. 13 of 1 March 2024. The governing law expressly authorizes:
- grants and subsidies;
- pilot-project financing;
- preferential loans.

The Fund may finance energy-efficiency measures, energy-management systems, smart meters/systems, cogeneration/high-efficiency cogeneration, renewable-energy use, and specified scientific/technical activity.

Primary sources:
- https://president.az/az/articles/view/52797
- https://president.az/az/articles/view/64875/print
- https://president.az/az/articles/view/64876

## 2026 implementation is live
The Ministry of Energy approved the detailed project requirements and application forms by Collegium Resolution No. 3-15/3-1-1/2026 dated 22 January 2026. AERA currently publishes dedicated pages for grants/subsidies/pilot projects and preferential loans.

Primary sources:
- https://minenergy.gov.az/az/energetika-nazirliyinin-normativ-huquqi-aktlari
- https://regulator.gov.az/az/enerji-effektivliyi-fondu/fond-haqqinda
- https://regulator.gov.az/az/stimullasdirici-tedbirler/pilot-layiheler-qrant-ve-subsidiyalar
- https://regulator.gov.az/az/stimullasdirici-tedbirler/guzestli-kreditler

The preferential-loan channel is concretely operational: AERA announced agreements with multiple banks in April 2026; the published lending terms are up to 5% annual interest, with project-size bands from 1,000 AZN to 10,000,000 AZN and maturities up to 5/10/15 years depending on size.

## Why this is not terminal SUCCESS
The governing rules do **not** create an unconditional fixed cash entitlement merely by spending money on an eligible project.

The project-financing process includes:
1. submission of a qualifying project;
2. project expertise/evaluation;
3. selection by the Agency;
4. financing only after a grant/subsidy contract is signed.

The annual financing ceiling for each measure is determined by the Agency. The 2026 project requirements also exclude multiple project classes, including land/building acquisition or rent, used-equipment reuse, weapons, alcohol, tobacco, certain hazardous materials and other excluded activities.

Therefore the state support is discretionary/selection-gated before contract signature. It cannot be treated as a guaranteed receivable ex ante.

## Exact guarantee test
For any candidate project define:

`worst_case_profit = locked_subsidy + locked_operating_savings + locked_sale_value - project_purchase_cost - installation - financing_cost - tax - maintenance - other_execution_costs`

A strict SUCCESS would require all of the following **before irreversible spend**:
- signed subsidy/grant contract fixing the amount or a binding calculation formula;
- all eligibility/technical gates satisfied;
- project purchase/install cost fixed;
- no clawback branch under compliant execution;
- all taxes/fees included;
- worst-case profit > 0 without relying on uncertain future energy prices, performance beyond guaranteed specs, or resale value.

No such fully locked current project was found in the public indexed sources during this packet.

## Important distinction
The preferential 5% loan is economically useful but is financing, not free cash. A lower interest rate alone cannot make a negative-NPV project a strict arbitrage unless another deterministic subsidy/savings/revenue leg exceeds all costs.

Grant/subsidy funding is stronger because it is non-repayable after award, but the award itself is not automatic. Thus the branch becomes potentially executable only **after a specific signed funding decision**, at which point procurement-price arbitrage or guaranteed energy-service-contract savings could be screened.

## Strategic result
H095 validates a genuine current Azerbaijan subsidy channel available to physical/legal persons and already operational in 2026, but it is not a standalone guaranteed-profit mechanism.

Best reopening condition:
- concrete AERA-approved project with signed subsidy/grant contract **before** supplier commitment; and
- fixed supplier price plus guaranteed savings/resale/other deterministic value producing a positive all-in floor.

Do not repeat generic searches for whether the Fund exists; that is now established. Future work should search for a specific awarded/awardable project where state support can be locked before capital commitment.
