# H340 — C&R Prizes free-phone acquisition-route screen

Date: 2026-08-28
State: **CLOSED — NOT SUCCESS**

## Question
H339 identified free telephone/digital entry as a potentially important escape from the postage/loss blocker that closed H334-H338. H340 checks C&R Prizes because its site currently displays a free-phone-entry footer alongside active raffles and instant-win products.

## Governing-term check
The operator's governing T&Cs are internally decisive. The HOW TO ENTER section says that to obtain an Entry Number there are **two available routes**: (1) paid online and (2) free postal. The free route is expressly defined by Schedule 1. Schedule 1 is titled **POSTAL ENTRY REQUIREMENTS** and specifies one postcard per entry.

A generic footer/template elsewhere on the same site says that a competition may be entered by phone and says to see Schedule 1 for free-phone entry. But Schedule 1 contains no phone-entry procedure. The operator's own How to Play page likewise describes the general routes as online or free postal and directs users to Schedule 1.

Therefore H340 does **not** treat the footer/template phone copy as a binding zero-cost acquisition right. Under the governing contract text, the established free-entry route remains postal.

## Instant-pool cross-check
A recovered C&R cash-instant product has a 1,000-ID universe and exactly 100 listed instant-prize identifiers:

- £500 cash x1
- £250 cash x2
- £100 cash x6
- £50 cash x5
- £15 site credit x7
- £10 site credit x30
- £5 site credit x49

Counts reconcile exactly: `1+2+6+5+7+30+49 = 100`.

Thus:
- withdrawable-cash IDs = **14**;
- site-credit IDs = **86**;
- zero-instant IDs = **900**;
- arithmetic inconclusive = **0**.

Even if the conflicting phone footer were provisionally granted, a random single allocated identifier has a legal £0 withdrawable-instant-cash branch because 900/1,000 IDs carry no listed instant prize. So this reference instant pool independently fails the strict single-entry cash-floor test.

Current active-draw snapshots also show an instant-win product with 1,000 total IDs; ticket-price snapshots disagree across crawls, so H340 deliberately does not use ticket price as a closure premise.

## Result
**C&R's generic phone-footer mechanism is closed as a rigorous free-phone acquisition lane under the currently published governing T&Cs.** This is not a global exhaustion result. It also does not prove that every future C&R raffle lacks a specially written phone route on its own raffle page; such a future page would need explicit competition-specific contractual language that overrides the present two-route structure.

## NEXT ACTION
Search for operators whose **governing competition terms themselves**, not footer/template copy, create a binding telephone or digital no-purchase route. Prioritize a live product where every reachable allocated ID has withdrawable cash, or where the exact entrant cap/bundle makes the worst-case sum strictly positive. Reject immediately if a zero-cash support branch remains reachable.
