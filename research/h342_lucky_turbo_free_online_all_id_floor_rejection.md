# H342 — Lucky Turbo apparent free-online all-ID floor rejection

Date: 2026-08-28
State: **CLOSED — NOT SUCCESS**

## Question
H341 closed finite-cap single-winner free-phone draws because an external winner preserves a £0 branch. H342 asks whether a live competition with a page-level free online presentation and a prize on every identifier can remove both the acquisition-cost and zero-win branches.

## Candidate
Lucky Turbo `Free Credit Raffle #28` is live through 31 August 2026. The page states FREE entry, one entry per person, 100 total tickets, and `Every ticket wins`.

Published prize vector:
- 1 x £20 Site Credit
- 5 x £1 Site Credit
- 5 x £0.50 Site Credit
- 10 x £0.20 Site Credit
- 79 x £0.10 Site Credit

Exact reconciliation:
- prize identifiers = 1 + 5 + 5 + 10 + 79 = **100**
- total identifiers = **100**
- zero-prize identifiers = **0**
- aggregate stated face value = **£37.40**
- minimum stated face value = **£0.10**

## Governing-route test
The competition page displays `ONLINE ENTRY` and `FREE POSTAL`, but the current governing Terms are narrower:
- the definition of `Entry` is a valid paid or free postal entry;
- `Free Entry` is defined as a postal entry submitted under the postal instructions;
- clause 3.1 says entry is either purchased via the Website or entered free by post.

Accordingly, page UI alone does not establish a contractual zero-cost online free route.

## Withdrawable-cash test
More importantly, the all-ID positive face-value floor is not a cash floor. Clause 7.1 says prizes cannot be exchanged for cash or an alternative unless expressly stated. Clause 7.4 says Site Credit is applied to the account and can only be used toward future draws.

The competition page separately says that if a prize cannot be supplied exactly as described, an equal-value cash alternative or replacement may be provided at the winner's preference. That is a conditional substitution clause triggered by prize unavailability; it is not an unconditional right to cash out ordinary Site Credit.

Thus every identifier has positive Site Credit face value, but the minimum guaranteed **withdrawable cash** is exactly **£0**.

## Result
**CLOSED / NOT SUCCESS.** This candidate does not satisfy the H341 NEXT ACTION. It teaches a stronger screening rule: `every ticket wins` must be normalized to withdrawable cash, and page-level `online/free` UI must be checked against the governing route definition before treating acquisition cost as zero.

Validation accounting:
- identifier reconciliation inconclusive: 0
- arithmetic inconclusive: 0
- route inconclusive relevant to closure: 0
- prize-convertibility inconclusive relevant to closure: 0

## NEXT ACTION
Search only for competitions whose governing rules themselves provide a binding zero-cost non-postal route and whose every reachable identifier pays withdrawable cash directly, or grants an unconditional cash election.
