## Exact deterministic cover theorem
Current official rules specify:
- number field = 1 through 80;
- exactly 20 winning numbers selected per drawing;
- 1 Spot, $1 wager, match 1 prize = **$3**;
- the $1 prize table scales proportionally with wager size;
- critically, the only pari-mutuel tier is the **10-Spot match-10** tier after the $2m liability cap; the rules state **all other tier payout values are fixed**.

Therefore a player who obtains one valid $1 1-Spot wager on each of the 80 numbers for the **same draw** has:

- face cost: `80 * $1 = $80`;
- exactly 20 winning tickets in every possible draw;
- fixed payout: `20 * $3 = $60`;
- strict deterministic gross ratio: `r = 60/80 = 0.75 = 75%`.

No probability estimate is used: this follows from the partition of the 80-number field into exactly 20 drawn and 60 undrawn numbers.

## Subsidy threshold
Let `q` be a deterministic face-price discount or externally funded free-play fraction that is **locked before external cash becomes irreversible**.

External cash cost is:

`C = 80 * (1-q)`.

Guaranteed gross payout is:

`G = 60`.

Positive pre-tax surplus requires:

`60 > 80(1-q)`

so

`q > 0.25`.

Thus **any player-owned, pre-committed subsidy strictly above 25% of the $80 face basket would mathematically cross break-even before tax/friction.**

Equivalent free-play form: if `F` dollars of the $80 basket is funded by already-locked qualifying free play, external cash is `$80-F` and guaranteed pre-tax profit is:

`P = 60 - (80-F) = F - 20`.

Therefore `F > $20` is the exact pre-tax threshold.

## Rewards-program screen
Current Rewards rules materially improve the data quality versus H141:

1. Keno is explicitly listed as an eligible computer-generated Rewards product.
2. Eligible computer-generated/retail draw purchases earn **5 points per $1 spent**.
3. **250 points** are required to redeem a coupon for a free play of a Lottery product.
4. A player may redeem at most **5,000 points/month for free play**.
5. Retail Rewards redemption may be a Lottery Vending Machine credit for an available game or a retail draw-game coupon.
6. Points can also be earned from Lottery engagement interactions, so pre-existing points need not all be generated by the target Keno basket.

However this does **not** establish a current >25% deterministic subsidy:
- the published 250-point rule defines coupon count, not a public fixed cash denomination for a Keno coupon;
- the exact playable value/spot/wager represented by a redeemed Keno coupon is not locked by the public text retrieved;
- Rewards points have no monetary/property value and program eligibility/redemption mechanics remain operator-controlled;
- same-spend points post after purchase (potentially up to 48 hours), so they cannot lower the external cash committed to the very same first cover unless recycled in later cycles.

For the first $80 retail cover, the ordinary purchase earns 400 points, enough for only one 250-point coupon plus remainder. Even before valuing that coupon, this is plainly not evidence of an immediate >$20 pre-committed subsidy to the first basket.

## Important pari-mutuel blocker eliminated
The public Keno page warns that prizes may become pari-mutuel under certain conditions. The official rules resolve this precisely: the liability cap and pari-mutuel conversion apply only to **10-Spot match-10**. Section H states that **all other tier payout values are fixed**.

Therefore external-player crowding does **not** reduce the $3 1-Spot payout. This is a major improvement over jackpot/full-space strategies whose guarantee fails on prize sharing.

## Execution gate
The official Keno rules also say:
- one Keno ticket contains one play;
- Keno tickets cannot be canceled;
- if a play is not accepted, Lottery/Retailer liability is limited to refund of the amount paid for that play;
- players must be present at the terminal for retail purchases;
- the Lottery terms reserve broad purchase/wager limitation powers.

So 80 separate 1-Spot plays must all be valid for the same draw before the deterministic $60 floor exists. With drawings every four minutes, retail execution speed/cutoff is nontrivial. A future terminal SUCCESS packet needs either:
- a batch/digital playslip/terminal method that demonstrably accepts all 80 selections for the same draw before the player is committed to an incomplete basket; or
- a purchase-level discount/free-play mechanism where partial rejection cannot create a net-negative committed position.

## Taxes and geography
The 75% theorem is pre-tax and mechanical. A terminal guarantee still requires:
- eligible Virginia purchase/claim execution;
- bounded tax treatment after wagering-loss rules;
- no hidden coupon restriction;
- all 80 tickets valid for the same drawing.
