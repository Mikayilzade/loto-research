## Why this packet is new

H331 found a finite all-cash pool with complete player cap but sub-break-even listed-price economics. H332 tests a stronger acquisition route: a finite all-cash Every Ticket Wins pool whose free postal route makes the arithmetic cross strict break-even if every identifier can actually be acquired.

The finished Win A Million `Cash Grab` game publishes:
- 10,000 total tickets;
- every ticket pays a real cash prize;
- guaranteed instant cash liabilities £16,719;
- separate guaranteed main draw prize £100;
- paid price £2 per ticket.

The complete advertised prize vector is:

- 12 x £50;
- 30 x £25;
- 75 x £10;

Counts reconcile exactly to 10,000 tickets and cash to £16,719, with zero missing identifiers and zero arithmetic-inconclusive classes.

## Postal arithmetic

Current Royal Mail 2nd Class standard-letter stamp price is £0.91 (effective 7 April 2026). Win A Million's current terms allow free postal entry by a separately posted unenclosed postcard; for Instant Win games each valid postal entry receives entries equal in value to £1 of paid entries or the selected game's entry price, whichever is higher. For this £2 game, the favorable interpretation is one £0.91 postcard -> one £2 Cash Grab entry.

If all 10,000 identifiers could be acquired by accepted second-class postal entries:

`10,000 x £0.91 = £9,100` postage cost.

Instant cash alone would be £16,719, and granting the £100 main draw gives £16,819. Therefore:

- gross/cost = **184.824176%**;
- deterministic surplus = **£7,719**;
- break-even accepted-entry cost = **£1.6819**.

This is a genuine >100% arithmetic crossing, unlike H331.

## Why it is not a strict guarantee

Current governing terms prevent certifying complete acquisition of this finite pool:

1. each postal entry must be a separate manually submitted postcard; bulk entries are invalid;
2. postal entries are processed within up to three working days rather than atomically on receipt;
3. if an Instant Win competition is closed or tickets are unavailable, a received postal entry can be allocated to the next available/equivalent game instead of the intended pool;
4. current Fair Play / Anti-Syndicate terms prohibit bulk/automated/exploitative activity and permit the promoter to void entries, withhold prizes, restrict participation, or close accounts where it reasonably believes a player is attempting unfair advantage;
5. therefore there is no enforceable pre-entry mechanism shown by the checked rules that reserves all 10,000 target identifiers for one entrant before outsiders can consume inventory or before the operator can redirect/reject entries.

A single unacquired target identifier is enough to destroy the claim that the complete payout vector is deterministically owned. The arithmetic is positive, but the execution premise needed for a strict guarantee is not established.
