# H098 — Azerbaijan statutory finder / treasure / archive rewards

Updated: 2026-08-19
Status: **MECHANISMS VALIDATED / STANDALONE GUARANTEE REJECTED OR EXECUTION-GATED / NOT SUCCESS**

## Goal
Screen current Azerbaijan non-reimbursement statutory rewards and transfer-like bounties where a person can receive deterministic cash/value after satisfying an objective legal condition, with emphasis on mechanisms that could in principle create a positive cash floor without lottery risk.

## 1. Ordinary lost-property finder reward — Civil Code Article 186
Current Civil Code text provides:
- a finder must promptly notify/return the lost item to the owner/authorized person or police;
- if the authorized person accepts the found item, the finder may demand a reward of **up to 5% of the item's value**;
- documented storage costs may also be recoverable;
- no reward right arises if the finder fails to report or tries to conceal the item.

Primary source:
- Azerbaijan Civil Code, Article 186, indexed by e-qanun: https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/0/c_c_8.html

### Guarantee test
This is a real statutory value-transfer mechanism, but it is not a controllable arbitrage:
- the qualifying event (lawful finding of somebody else's lost property) is stochastic and cannot be manufactured without creating legal/contractual risk;
- the statute says **up to 5%**, so the maximum is bounded but the exact pre-event payment floor is not fixed at 5%;
- any deliberate purchase of an item from a willing seller is not a `tapıntı` and does not trigger Article 186.

Result: **REJECTED as standalone guaranteed-profit path**. Preserve only as a legal-bounty class control.

## 2. Treasure (`dəfinə`) — Civil Code Article 187
Current Civil Code text provides:
- ordinary ownerless hidden treasure normally belongs 50/50 to the property owner and lawful finder unless they agree otherwise;
- if the treasure is historically/culturally/scientifically valuable, it must be transferred to the state;
- in that cultural-treasure branch, the property owner + finder together have a right to a reward equal to **50% of the treasure's value**;
- absent another agreement, that reward is split equally;
- unauthorized digging/search transfers the reward to the property owner rather than the finder.

Primary sources:
- e-qanun Civil Code Article 187: https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/0/c_c_8.html
- current supporting monument law: https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/3/c_f_3526.html
- 2026 amendment confirms failure to report/hand over qualifying discovered monuments/treasure is punishable: https://president.az/az/articles/view/71428

### Guarantee test
The 50% statutory reward is economically substantial but still fails the project's terminal guarantee criterion:
- discovery is stochastic;
- lawful search requires the property owner's consent;
- before discovery there is no locked qualifying asset or appraised value;
- buying/searching land or paying search costs creates positive irreversible cost while the no-treasure outcome remains legal;
- unauthorized search destroys the finder's entitlement.

A future executable variant would require a **pre-existing already-discovered object**, owner consent, official classification/appraisal locked before any acquisition payment, and a contract allocating enough of the statutory 50% reward to the buyer/searcher so that `locked reward - acquisition - tax/fees > 0`. Public sources reviewed do not supply such a live transaction.

Result: **MECHANISM VALIDATED / EXECUTION-GATED / NOT SUCCESS**.

## 3. Valuable discovered archive documents — full expert-value reward
A stronger and more unusual rule is currently listed by the Azerbaijan National Archive Administration itself in its legislation directory (page updated 2026-05-13).

Under Cabinet of Ministers Decision No. 32 of 6 March 2000, the `Aşkar edilmiş və ya tapılmış arxiv sənədlərini ... mükafatlandırma Qaydası` states:
- valuable archive documents relating to Azerbaijani history/culture found in Azerbaijan or abroad can qualify;
- specialists/experts review the documents;
- an Expert-Valuation Commission determines the historical-document value;
- the **full amount of the price determined by the Commission** is paid to the finder/discoverer as the reward;
- payment is made by the National Archive Administration from budget funds allocated for this purpose.

Primary sources:
- current National Archive legislation index (updated 2026-05-13): https://milliarxiv.gov.az/az/qerarlar
- e-qanun consolidated Decision No. 32, especially clauses 5–7 of the finder-reward rules: https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/0/c_f_867.html

This is stronger than a random prize because the reward is tied to an official appraisal and the rule says the appraised price is paid **in full**.

### Arbitrage test
Potential construction:
1. identify a privately held historical document/collection offered below expected archival value;
2. obtain a pre-acquisition official or contractually reliable valuation/eligibility determination;
3. acquire it only if total cost is below the locked reward;
4. transfer it under the reward rule.

Terminal problem: the public rule does **not** provide a pre-purchase binding valuation service or a quoted guaranteed price for a candidate document. The valuation decision occurs after the document is presented/reviewed. Therefore an acquisition made before valuation retains a legal branch where the Commission values the item at or below cost or rejects it as non-qualifying.

Necessary condition for strict SUCCESS:

`binding pre-acquisition archival reward R - purchase price P - taxes/fees/transport F > 0`

with eligibility, ownership/provenance, and payout entitlement all verified before irreversible payment.

No current public live candidate with this locked sequence was found in this packet.

Result: **STRONG STATUTORY BOUNTY MECHANISM VALIDATED / PRE-VALUATION EXECUTION-GATED / NOT SUCCESS**.

## 4. Battery-collection control
The Ministry of Ecology's active 2026 `Batareyaları təhvil ver, təbiəti qoru` project continues collecting used batteries and transferring them to the Waste Management Center, including 221 kg reported on 7 May 2026 and 83 kg on 19 June 2026. The official notices describe environmental collection/utilization, not a cash reward to contributors.

Sources:
- https://www.eco.gov.az/az/nazirlik/xeber?newsID=29791
- https://www.eco.gov.az/az/nazirlik/xeber?newsID=30445

Result: **REJECTED as bounty/cash-redemption path on current public terms**.

## General theorem for H098
A statutory reward of face/appraised value does not itself create guaranteed profit. For a purchase-then-redeem construction, all of these must be locked before irreversible cost:
1. the asset is legally acquirable and transferable;
2. the claimant is eligible;
3. the redemption/reward amount has a binding lower bound `R_min`;
4. total acquisition + transaction + tax cost has a binding upper bound `C_max`;
5. no legal branch allows denial/clawback after compliant execution;
6. `R_min > C_max`.

H098 found three genuine Azerbaijan legal reward classes, but none currently passes the pre-cost locking requirement.

## Strategic conclusion
- Ordinary finder reward: **closed standalone**.
- Cultural treasure 50% reward: **execution-gated, not a controllable guaranteed strategy**.
- Archive-document full expert-value reward: **strongest new lead**, but only if a binding valuation/eligibility decision can be obtained before purchase.
- 2026 battery collection: no public cash reward found.

Next high-value search should target **programs with published fixed redemption schedules or pre-approval certificates**, not discretionary/appraisal-after-acquisition awards. This includes cultural-property purchase schedules, state museum acquisition programs, recycling buybacks, surrender/bounty programs, and transferable public entitlements where the payment amount can be locked before acquisition.
