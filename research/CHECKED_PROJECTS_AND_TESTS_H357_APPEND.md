## H357 — UK NS&I Premium Bonds

Mechanism tested: principal-preserving lottery/savings product with externally funded random prizes.

Result: **closed for strict guaranteed profit under current structure**.

At the legal maximum £50,000 holding, the August 2026 draw had 136,946,390,805 eligible £1 Bonds and 6,224,837 prizes. Even if every prize were forced onto a distinct Bond number, 136,940,115,968 eligible external Bonds would remain after assigning every prize outside our holding. Therefore a zero-prize branch exists.

Because principal can be withdrawn without penalty and is HM Treasury-backed, the nominal deterministic gross floor is exactly 100% of principal, but the strict profit floor is £0 rather than >£0.

Reopen criterion: guaranteed per-holder interest/bonus/prize above principal, or a finite eligible ID universe that can be monopolized under the holding cap.
