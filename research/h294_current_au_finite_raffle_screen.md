# H294 — Current Australian finite-raffle takeover screen

Updated: 2026-08-26

## Question
Can a currently available hard-capped raffle be turned into a strict guaranteed-profit construction by buying the entire finite identifier pool?

## Method
Use a deliberately stronger-than-real model: assume one player can acquire every ticket identifier and therefore receives every advertised prize liability that remains relevant. Ignore execution friction, eligibility conflicts, sellout competition, valuation discounts, and any restriction that would hurt the player. If total player-facing liabilities are still below full-pool acquisition cost, that pool is closed by arithmetic alone.

The best case is yourtown Draw 559, and even it returns only 42.84% under impossible-perfect ownership.

- RSPCA Lottery Draw 92: https://www.rspcalottery.com.au/rspca-lottery/terms-and-conditions — 160,000-ticket cap, A$10 ticket, A$320,000 main prize package and A$3,000 multi-ticket bonus draw.
- MS QLD Cash Grab Draw 2: https://www.msqlotteries.com.au/cash-grab — 10,000 tickets, A$5 each, A$10,000 cash first prize.

## Conservative / player-favourable choices
For yourtown 559 the arithmetic counts A$15k Kids Helpline Week, A$5k Last Chance, A$10k 2nd Chance and A$50k member bonus in addition to the A$2.94m first prize. Some of these have eligibility/channel conditions, so counting all of them only strengthens the takeover hypothesis. The already-drawn Early Bird prize is not needed; adding it would not materially change the rejection.

For Prestige Car 1158, all listed A$5k/A$2k bonus liabilities are counted together with the A$260k first prize even though real eligibility can be narrower.

## Conclusion
**CLOSED / REJECTED.** None of these four current hard-capped pools can yield strict guaranteed profit by complete identifier takeover. The rejection is stronger than an execution blocker: even impossible-perfect ownership with generous prize aggregation leaves a large arithmetic deficit.

Reopen only after a material change in ticket cap, ticket price, prize liabilities, or a deterministic external subsidy sufficient to move full-takeover liabilities above 100% of exact acquisition cost.
