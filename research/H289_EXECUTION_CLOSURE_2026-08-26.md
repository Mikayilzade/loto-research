## Consequence for the H289 exact covers
H289's conditional constructions require all members of the cover to be acquired for the same Keno drawing.

- Spot-1 cover: all 80 single-number plays. If any required number is refused or limited, there is a legal 20-number draw containing only uncovered/under-covered required mass sufficient to destroy the claimed deterministic $40 floor.
- Spot-2 six-clique cover: likewise depends on acquiring every planned pair. Selective refusal of one or more required pairs destroys the exact combinatorial floor certificate.

Because the governing online terms expressly preserve selective refusal/limitation, complete acquisition is not guaranteed by the rules. This is a terminal execution blocker independent of whether the August Refer-A-Friend award stacks with the first-deposit match.
