# H065b — 2026 fallow subsidy cashability and current-entry closure

Updated: 2026-08-18
Status: **mechanism validated; new-entry 2026 standalone guarantee rejected**

## Question
Can a new entrant on 2026-08-18 create a strictly positive cash-floor by acquiring/using land solely to claim Azerbaijan's new 2026 fallow-land subsidy?

## Official rule set
The Agricultural Subsidy Council's 18 August 2025 decision establishes a 2026 fallow subsidy of **220 AZN/ha** only for rain-fed parcels in **Shamakhi, Sheki, Gobustan and Yardimli** where wheat or barley had been planted and declared in EKTIS in each of the preceding three consecutive years. Other cases receive coefficient zero.

Primary sources:
- https://www.agro.gov.az/index.php/az/news/010920254
- https://www.akia.gov.az/az/content/255-281.html

AKIA's current FAQ defines fallow subsidy as a subsidy paid per hectare for a parcel left fallow for one year after the required consecutive cultivation period. The same FAQ explicitly includes **fallow land** in the electronic declaration/application data required for planting subsidy.

Primary source:
- https://www.akia.gov.az/az/content/24.html
- https://akia.gov.az/az/content/11-24.html

## Cashability gate — resolved
AKIA's current 2025–2026 payment guidance states that subsidy money is transferred to the Farmer Card and:
- **25% may be used as cash**;
- **75% is non-cash restricted agricultural-use value**.

Primary source:
- https://www.akia.gov.az/az/opennews/390.html

Because fallow subsidy is expressly defined within the planting-subsidy application framework, the strongest current interpretation is that the standard Farmer Card split applies absent a fallow-specific exception.

For 220 AZN/ha:
- cash component = **55 AZN/ha**;
- restricted component = **165 AZN/ha**.

For the project's terminal cash-guarantee test, the 165 AZN restricted balance must not be counted as cash profit unless it offsets an independently required agricultural purchase at full economic value.

## Current-entry gate — resolved against a new 2026 entrant
AKIA states ordinary declaration/application windows are:
- autumn crops: 1 September through the last working day of December;
- spring crops: 1 February through 1 June / May 31 in the detailed guidance;
- repeat crops: 1 June through 31 August.

It also states that fallow land is part of the electronic declaration/application set and that late declarations do not generate the subsidy application/payment.

Primary sources:
- https://akia.gov.az/az/opennews/146.html
- https://www.akia.gov.az/az/content/24.html
- https://akia.gov.az/az/content/20-83.html

The 2026 fallow coefficient itself is explicitly a **2026** measure. On 2026-08-18, the qualifying 2026 autumn and spring entry periods are already past. The still-open repeat-crop period is a different category and does not create the required 2026 fallow entitlement.

More importantly, purchasing or leasing a parcel today cannot manufacture eligibility: the parcel itself must already carry the required **three consecutive prior years of wheat/barley EKTIS declarations**. Land-use documents are part of the application and verification process.

Therefore a person starting from zero on 2026-08-18 cannot create a fresh 2026 fallow-subsidy right merely by buying/leasing land now.

## Guarantee theorem
For a new entrant today, at least one required state is unavailable before capital commitment:
1. qualifying three-year parcel history;
2. timely 2026 fallow declaration/application;
3. accepted monitoring/land documentation.

Thus the current strategy cannot be initiated now with a guaranteed positive cash floor.

For a farmer who already timely applied on an already-qualifying parcel, the cash entitlement may still be economically positive:

`cash component = 55 AZN/ha`

but this is an already-created/vested agricultural entitlement, not a currently reproducible new-entry arbitrage. Any acquisition price, rent, compliance cost or foregone crop margin would also have to be below the 55 AZN/ha cash component for a cash-only guarantee.

## Result
- 220 AZN/ha fallow mechanism: **VALIDATED**.
- Standard cashable fraction: **25% / 55 AZN per ha**, strongly supported by current AKIA payment rules.
- 75% restricted value: **do not count as standalone cash profit**.
- New 2026 entry on 2026-08-18: **REJECTED** because eligibility is path-dependent and the relevant ordinary application windows have passed.
- Existing timely applicants: potentially positive subsidy receipt, but **not a reproducible new-entry guaranteed-profit strategy**.

## Reopen rule
Reopen H065 only if one of the following appears:
- a 2027 fallow coefficient/program with a future declaration window;
- an official late-entry/amendment route applicable to fallow declarations;
- a transferable already-approved fallow entitlement whose acquisition cost can be locked below guaranteed cash redemption.
