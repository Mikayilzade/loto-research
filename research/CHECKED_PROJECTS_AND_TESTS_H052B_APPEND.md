## General-contract recheck
Expressbank's published general terms confirm insurance mechanics and state that deposit interest normally accrues until return/removal of principal. They do not contain a product-specific rule for advance-interest recoupment on an insurance event. The general document therefore cannot prove `C=P` for `Əlavə fürsət`.

Current Deposit Insurance Law Article 26.2 remains decisive: if the depositor has a **matured obligation** to the participant bank on the insurance-event date, the unperformed amount is deducted from the protected deposit before compensation.

Primary law: https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/12/f_12137.html

The Central Bank's current Q&A independently restates the Article 26.2 offset rule and points consumers to current bank product conditions; it does not publish an exception for prepaid-interest products.

Primary source: https://cbar.az/page-73/questions-and-answers

## Contract-architecture control
A real Azerbaijani upfront-interest deposit contract indexed publicly for another bank/product shows a materially adverse architecture: the advance is credited to the customer, an equivalent portion of principal is blocked/encumbered, and on customer early withdrawal the prepaid interest is deducted from principal. This is **not evidence that Expressbank or VTB use the same clause**, but it proves that apparently `insured + upfront interest` marketing alone is insufficient to infer full insured-principal recovery.

Because current law now turns specifically on whether a matured depositor obligation exists at the insurance event, product wording is not a cosmetic detail; it is the terminal variable.

## Result
**H052 public-web path is DATA-BLOCKED, not SUCCESS and not rejected.**

The repeated broad-search step is now closed. Current public official web sources establish the product mechanism but do not publish the one contract clause required for a strict guarantee theorem.

Do not repeat generic searches for `Avans / Əlavə fürsət + müqavilə / Ərizə-Razılaşma` unless a new document route appears.

Any one of the following is sufficient to reopen:
