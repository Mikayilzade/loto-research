# H119 append — EuroDreams Ireland guaranteed-winner promotion

Updated: 2026-08-20

Official current EuroDreams structure: 6/40 plus Dream Number 1/5; 19,191,900 possible lines at €2.50 each. The Ireland-only event adds one extra €2,000/month for 5 years prize to one normal Irish winner.

Full-space cost: €47,979,750.

Even under deliberately favorable assumptions — full undiscounted nominal value for Tier 1 (€7.2m), four Tier 2 annuities (€120k each), displayed average lower-tier prizes treated as exact, and the entire extra promotional annuity (€120k) granted to the full-space buyer — total gross is only €21,431,110, or 44.66699% of spend.

The promotion itself contributes at most about 0.2501% of full-space spend.

Status: **REJECTED guaranteed-profit full coverage / fixed winner-top-up far too small to bridge takeout**.

Sources checked 2026-08-20: Irish National Lottery EuroDreams game information and EuroDreams Event pages.
