# H097 — Azerbaijan self-employment asset-package residual-value screen

Updated: 2026-08-19
Status: **PUBLIC PACKAGE COMPOSITION VALIDATED / STRONG LEAD NARROWED / NOT SUCCESS**

## Goal
Continue H096 by screening the officially published 2026 self-employment asset packages for the best chance of a positive worst-case residual-value transfer after the required one-year compliant business period.

This packet does **not** assume resale immediately after delivery. Official rules say assets are provided under contract for one year, the participant is monitored, and ownership transfers only if activity at the end of the contract matches the submitted business plan. If the participant unjustifiably fails to use the property for a year, the property or its then-market value must be returned.

## Screening rule
For a package to reach terminal SUCCESS through residual-value transfer, all of the following must be locked **before any irreversible participant cost**:

`conservative post-transfer realizable asset value + guaranteed operating cash inflows - participant cash costs - taxes/fees - required one-year compliance costs > 0`

for every permitted outcome under the signed contract.

Public package composition alone is insufficient because the official pages do not fix:
- exact brands/models/serial specifications for most assets;
- minimum allowed business activity during the one-year monitoring period;
- required premises/rent/utilities/insurance/maintenance;
- failure/replacement risk allocation in the exact participant contract;
- a guaranteed post-transfer buyer price.

Therefore even a clearly valuable package can still have a negative worst-case one-year cash branch.

## Priority ranking for future execution
1. **Small photo studio** — portable/liquid assets; likely low operating infrastructure.
2. **Phone repair** — portable durable equipment; low-to-moderate fixed infrastructure.
3. **Printing services** — portable assets, but consumables and printer depreciation matter.
4. **Auto mechanic / wheel alignment** — potentially highest gross asset value, but premises/installation/utility burden can dominate the guarantee test.
5. Car cleaning / other workshop packages — lower priority because business-plan compliance likely carries unavoidable site/utility costs.

## Conclusion
H096's self-employment asset-transfer lead is real and is now narrowed to specific official packages. The best public-data candidates are **photo studio, phone repair and printing** for low infrastructure burden, while **auto mechanic / wheel alignment** may have higher gross asset value but much higher execution-cost uncertainty.

No package can be called a guaranteed-profit construction from public data alone. Reopen H097 only when there is a specific eligible/approved participant, signed contract/business plan, exact delivered models, a one-year minimum-cost budget and a conservative binding or highly defensible post-transfer liquidation value.
