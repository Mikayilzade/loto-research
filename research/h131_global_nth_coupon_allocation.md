# H131 — global `Nth` lottery coupon allocation / deterministic tranche test

Updated: 2026-08-20
Status: **CONDITIONAL CASH INVERSION VALIDATED / PLAYER-OWNED TRANCHE GUARANTEE REJECTED / NO SUCCESS**

## Question
Can an official lottery promotion that awards a cash coupon to every `Nth` qualifying ticket create a deterministic block/tranche arbitrage? This is the highest-value escape from the external-ticket zero-floor problem if a player can own a complete reward-bearing tranche.

## Primary examples — Florida Lottery 2026
Florida Lottery ran several official promotions using statewide `Nth` allocation:

1. **FLORIDA LOTTO Love (Jan 30–Feb 28, 2026)**
   - qualifying purchase: FLORIDA LOTTO ticket containing 52 Advance Play draws;
   - FLORIDA LOTTO base price: $2 per play/draw;
   - qualifying one-play 52-draw ticket cost = **$104**;
   - instant prize = **$208 cash prize coupon**;
   - coupon prints automatically with every `Nth` qualifying FLORIDA LOTTO ticket purchased **statewide**.
   - Sources: Florida Lottery Lotto Love page; current FLORIDA LOTTO game/FAQ pages; Florida FAC chapter confirms rule 53ER26-10 existed/effective 2026-01-30.

2. **PICK Midday (May 1–31, 2026)**
   - qualifying purchase: any $1+ PICK Daily Games ticket during 6am–1pm ET;
   - instant prize: **$5 cash coupon**;
   - **200,000** coupons / **$1,000,000** total;
   - coupon prints with every `Nth` qualifying ticket purchased **statewide**.
   - Sources: Florida Lottery May 1, 2026 release and promotion page; rule 53ER26-24.

3. **FANTASY 5 More Money March (Mar 1–31, 2026)**
   - qualifying purchase: $5+ FANTASY 5 ticket;
   - instant prize: **$10 cash coupon**;
   - coupon prints with every `Nth` qualifying ticket purchased **statewide**.
   - Source: Florida Lottery Feb 27, 2026 release; rule 53ER26-17.

4. **X THE CASH / 500X THE CASH (2026)**
   - $50 scratch ticket, and only non-winning 500X tickets are coupon-eligible after entry;
   - **3,000 × $100 cash coupons**;
   - official rule says coupons are **randomly generated and awarded for every `Nth` non-winning ticket entered statewide** and that odds depend on the number of tickets entered.
   - Source: Florida rule 53ER26-1 / official X THE CASH rules.

These are not generic random second-chance drawings: the cash amount is fixed and the allocation is linked to an `Nth` statewide sequence.

## Strong conditional inversion
The most striking case is Lotto Love.

For one base FLORIDA LOTTO play across 52 draws:

`purchase cost = 52 × $2 = $104`

`Nth cash coupon = $208`

If the player could **guarantee** ownership of an Nth slot, the coupon alone would give:

`guaranteed coupon-only gross = $208 / $104 = 200%`

`coupon-only net = +$104`

before valuing any prizes from the 52 underlying lottery draws. Therefore this is a genuine **conditional deterministic cash inversion**; unlike most promotions, the blocker is not payout size.

Analogously:
- PICK Midday: minimum $1 qualifying ticket -> $5 coupon => +$4 conditional coupon-only net;
- FANTASY 5 More Money March: $5 qualifying ticket -> $10 coupon => +$5 conditional coupon-only net;
- 500X THE CASH: a known non-winning $50 ticket that receives the $100 coupon would recover $50 above original ticket cost, but coupon assignment is not controllable.

## Global-Nth interleaving theorem
Let qualifying statewide purchase/entry positions be positive integers. Suppose coupons occur at a hidden or rule-defined sequence such as

`W = {r, r+N, r+2N, ...}`

for some phase `r`, or are otherwise generated by the operator at `Nth` events. A player makes a finite set of purchases `P`, but other eligible players may make arbitrarily many purchases between the player's transactions.

**Claim:** absent exclusive control of every statewide qualifying position over a complete reward interval, the player's strict coupon floor is zero.

Reason:
1. A coupon belongs to a qualifying **statewide position**, not to a player-local block.
2. External qualifying purchases may occur between any two player purchases.
3. For any finite player purchase plan that does not lock every statewide position, there is a legal interleaving in which each coupon-bearing position is occupied by an external purchase.
4. Therefore a player cannot prove `P ∩ W != empty` for every lawful sales/entry sequence.
5. With the stronger X THE CASH wording — coupons are `randomly generated` and odds depend on total entries — even a simple known fixed-phase model is not supported by the rules.

Thus purchasing `N` personal tickets does **not** imply one coupon. `Every Nth statewide` is not the same as `one coupon per player-owned block of N`.

## What would convert this class to SUCCESS?
Any future promotion becomes a high-priority candidate if primary rules provide at least one of:

1. **player-local counter:** every Nth ticket purchased by the same account/player gets the fixed reward;
2. **retailer-local counter + exclusive terminal batch:** a buyer can contractually lock all N sequential qualifying transactions at a terminal and external terminals cannot share the counter;
3. **explicit block guarantee:** each purchased pack/block of N gets at least one cash coupon;
4. **ring-fenced tranche:** specified serial range or tranche is guaranteed a known minimum cash allocation and can be purchased completely;
5. **observable phase + atomic batch:** next winning counter position is publicly/contractually known and the player can atomically acquire it before any external entry.

None of the checked 2026 Florida examples provides such player-owned allocation. Their wording is statewide, chance-based, and in X THE CASH explicitly random/entry-dependent.

## Result
H131 validates that deterministic lottery promotion subsidies **large enough to invert ticket cost really do exist** — Lotto Love is the cleanest proof, with a $208 coupon attached to a $104 qualifying Advance Play purchase when the coupon hits.

However, the allocation mechanism is global/statewide rather than player-owned. External interleaving leaves a lawful zero-coupon outcome for any finite player's purchase sequence. Therefore the current `Nth` coupon class does **not** establish guaranteed positive net profit.

This materially narrows NEXT ACTION: future block/tranche searches should prioritize **player-local, serial-range, retailer-local exclusive, or explicit one-per-block guarantees**, not generic statewide `Nth` promotions.

No terminal SUCCESS.
