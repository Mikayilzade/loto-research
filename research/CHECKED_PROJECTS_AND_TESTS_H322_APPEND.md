## SOBO Competitions — Instant Win Frenzy — 29p Tickets

**Status:** CLOSED / CAP-AND-RANDOM-ALLOCATION-BLOCKED.

Checked as a new finite-pool subsidy candidate because the live product combines a 500,000-ticket cap, a first ticket free, electronic checkout holds and near-zero sold inventory.

Key result: governing rules allocate numbers randomly and prohibit selecting a specific identifier. Even under the player-favourable assumption that all 50,000 advertised instant-credit prizes and five £1,000 cash prizes occupy distinct IDs, at least **449,995** zero-instant IDs remain. This is far above the **2,000** per-person cap, so a legal allocation gives the player's entire allowed portfolio no instant prize. The open main draw also preserves external-winner states.

Strict withdrawable-cash floor: **£0**.

Sources checked:
- https://sobocompetitions.com/competitions/instant-win-29p
- https://sobocompetitions.com/competition-rules
- https://sobocompetitions.com/terms
- https://sobocompetitions.com/free-entry

Reusable lesson: a deterministic ticket discount does not create a guaranteed-profit mechanism when ticket IDs are random/hidden and the residual zero-cash set is at least as large as the player's maximum allocation.
