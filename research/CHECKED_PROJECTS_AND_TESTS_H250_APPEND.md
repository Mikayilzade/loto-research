# H250 append — partial digit courier subsidy floor

- **Class:** courier-supported exact partial-digit partitions × deterministic credits/discounts.
- **Current state evidence:** Maine/Vermont/Minnesota Pick 3 expose exact-position one-digit wagers; Maine Pick 4 does too.
- **Courier evidence:** Lotto.com Maine directly exposes First/Second/Third Digit, 1:10, $5 prize on $1 minimum, and up to 100 lines/game.
- **Exact result:** all tested Single Digit / Pair / Straight exact partitions return 50% deterministically before subsidy.
- **Best capital requirement:** retail-style $0.50 Single Digit cover = $5 cost → exactly $2.50 gross. Lotto.com $1 minimum = $10 cost → exactly $5 gross.
- **Threshold:** percentage subsidy must be strictly >50% before any positive fee. Fixed credit threshold is `B > 2.50+F+A` for the $5 cover or `B > 5+F+A` for Lotto.com's $10 cover.
- **Current promotions:** Lotto.com Mystery Scratch rewards are random; its listed 20% draw / 10% all discounts do not cross the threshold. Previously tested 25% draw discount also fails. Strong Jackpocket $5/$10 credit offers recovered are historical/expired or offer-dependent/discretionary, not a current strict entitlement.
- **Result:** **NOT SUCCESS / DATA-BLOCKED on a current deterministic >50% subsidy**.
- **Reopen trigger:** current courier offer with deterministic fixed credit/discount crossing the exact threshold after all fees/acquisition costs, in a jurisdiction where the partial-digit menu is directly orderable.
