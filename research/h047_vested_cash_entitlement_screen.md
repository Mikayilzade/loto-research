# H047 — vested-cash entitlement / rebate screen

Updated: 2026-08-16
Status: **mechanism class exists; no current Azerbaijan-executable strict guarantee found**

## Goal
Search for a cash or cash-like subsidy that becomes contractually vested independently of the later wager result and therefore can repair the zero-profit whole-market-void branch established in H046.

Terminal SUCCESS requires strictly positive net profit in every reachable branch. A rebate that is earned only after a wager settles cannot repair a branch in which the qualifying wager itself is voided.

## Necessary-condition theorem
Let `A` be the ordinary arbitrage profit from a fully matched market, `R` a promotional cash/rebate payment, and `V` a reachable whole-market-void state.

If the promotion defines `R=0` whenever the qualifying wager is void/unsettled, then in state `V`:

`profit(V) = 0 + 0 = 0`.

Therefore such a promotion cannot produce terminal SUCCESS, regardless of how profitable the normal settled branch is.

To repair H046, the subsidy must instead satisfy all of:
1. cash/withdrawable value, not points, odds boosts or restricted free bets;
2. vested before the risky wager branch that may later void/cancel;
3. no requirement that the qualifying wager settle to preserve the already-vested cash;
4. no operator discretion/anti-arbitrage clause that can rescind the vested payment for the intended strategy;
5. accessible and lawful for the target user/jurisdiction;
6. amount exceeds all unavoidable fees/funding/FX costs while remaining strictly positive after every branch.

## Current official candidates

### BETDAQ DAQBACK — real withdrawable cash, but post-settlement commission dependent
Current BETDAQ promotion page advertises up to £1,000 of first-month commission back as **withdrawable funds**, with standard 2% commission applying and API/RDT customers excluded.

Source checked 2026-08-16:
- https://promotions.betdaq.com/daqback/

This is important because it validates the requested product class: exchange rebates can be paid as withdrawable cash rather than points.

However it does not solve H046. Commission is generated by valid settled trading; a whole-market void removes the settled winning-market commission whose rebate would be based on it. Hence the same reachable void branch produces neither arbitrage profit nor qualifying commission cashback.

Result: **VALIDATED cash-rebate mechanism; REJECTED as strict guarantee repair**.

### BETDAQ Healthy Betting cashback — real-money loss refund, but settlement dependent
BETDAQ's Healthy Betting terms state that eligible sportsbook/casino net losses receive 10% cashback, credited as real money. The same terms specify that cancelled/void bets are not eligible and that the operator may alter/discontinue the promotion under its general terms.

Source checked 2026-08-16:
- https://promotions.betdaq.com/healthy-betting/

Thus a void branch again creates no qualifying loss and no cashback. In addition, the loss refund is only 10%, so it is not a deterministic full principal refund.

Result: **REJECTED as strict guarantee repair**.

### Betfair Azerbaijan risk-free welcome offer — jurisdiction fit but contingent refund
Betfair's international/Azerbaijan offer explicitly lists Azerbaijan among eligible countries and refunds a qualifying first Exchange bet only if it loses; unmatched, unsettled or voided bets do not count.

Sources checked 2026-08-16:
- https://www.betfair.com/rs
- https://www.betfair.com/ge

This is geographically useful but fails the same theorem: a void qualifying bet does not create the refund.

Result: **REJECTED as strict guarantee repair; relevant Azerbaijan-accessible contingent subsidy**.

### Smarkets SailGP predictor — historical proof of a stronger zero-stake cash mechanism
A 2026 Smarkets SailGP Portsmouth promotion paid a £50 cash credit for a correct prediction and £25 even for a wrong prediction. No monetary wager was required to make the prediction; a £10 deposit was required only to bet or withdraw. This is substantially stronger than ordinary free-bet promotions because the reward floor was positive regardless of prediction outcome.

Source checked 2026-08-16:
- https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/37758027402781-Smarkets-SailGP-Portsmouth-Win-Predictor-Game

But it is not a current executable target:
- limited to new UK/Ireland users;
- sign-up deadline was 2026-08-02;
- cash credit expired after seven days absent qualifying account/deposit conditions;
- Smarkets retained discretion to exclude/withdraw/limit promotion availability.

Result: **historical mechanism proof only; not current Azerbaijan-executable SUCCESS**.

## Strategic conclusion
H047 separates two classes that had previously been mixed together:

1. **post-settlement rebates/refunds** — common and sometimes withdrawable cash, but they inherit the wager's void branch and therefore cannot repair H046;
2. **pre-wager / zero-stake unconditional cash entitlement** — the correct target class. A historical Smarkets promotion demonstrates this class can exist, but no current Azerbaijan-executable version has been found.

Therefore H047 does **not** reach SUCCESS, but it materially narrows the remaining search. Future scans should focus only on promotions/contracts where a strictly positive cash amount is owed after actions completely under the user's control and before any event-dependent or voidable wager.
