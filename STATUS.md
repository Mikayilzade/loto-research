## Azerbaijan statutory deposit protection — validated principal-floor mechanism
Current official ADIF material establishes:
- protected-deposit rate ceiling **12% AZN / 2.5% foreign currency**;
- compensation **100% up to 100,000 AZN per participating bank**;
- compensation includes interest accrued only through the insurance-event date, subject to the cap.

Current bank controls include:
- Kapital Bank digital deposits up to the protected-rate ceilings;
- Bank Respublika fixed maturity menus up to 10.5% in the sampled current product;
- XalqKart current July–December 2026 campaign with 7% annual return on eligible AZN balances and free card issuance.

## H051 terminal result
A new necessary-condition failure is now formalized:

For a protected deposit with principal `P`, accrued interest `I(t)` and costs `C`, an insurance event at `tau -> 0+` gives worst-case protected terminal value `P + I(tau) - C`, while `inf I(tau)=0` for ordinary post-opening accrual.

Therefore **deposit insurance + ordinary fixed annual interest does not by itself prove strictly positive profit in every allowed outcome**. It strongly protects principal, but immediate issuer failure can reduce the protected reward toward zero.

A separate current scan of Azerbaijan-facing bank/e-money/broker promotions found purchase cashback, bonus points, random/gamified rewards, fee waivers and trading/referral bonuses, but no current product was found combining statutory principal protection with an unconditional separately vested withdrawable cash reward that survives all branches.

Status: **NO SUCCESS; H051 ordinary insured-interest path REJECTED as terminal guarantee, stronger prepaid/upfront-reward branch remains open.**
