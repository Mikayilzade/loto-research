# H101 — prefunded execution-service deposit claim assignment: STRONGEST CURRENT LEAD
Files:
- `research/h101_prefunded_execution_service_deposit_claim_assignment.md`
- `research/CHECKED_PROJECTS_AND_TESTS_H101_APPEND.md`

Key mechanics validated from current Azerbaijan law:
1. `İcra haqqında` Article 31 expressly provides procedural substitution when a claim is assigned (`tələb güzəşt edildikdə`).
2. Article 81-1 requires enforcement-document money to move through the state enforcement-service deposit account, tied to the specific enforcement case number.
3. Article 81-1.3 routes deposited claimant money to the claimant's supplied bank account.
4. Article **74.1-1** now strengthens the timing: money already transferred/collected and payable to the claimant must be transferred to the claimant's bank account within **3 working days**.
5. Therefore a buyer can in principle acquire an **already-funded** enforcement claim, be substituted as claimant, and then rely on a short statutory payout clock once the amount is legally payable.

Strict theorem:
`cleared distributable deposit - surviving deductions/defenses - purchase price - transfer/tax/bank costs - residual reversal reserve > 0`.

Atomic closing requirement:
`verify cleared funds -> verify distributable amount/no diversion -> execute assignment -> enforcement officer issues Art.31 substitution -> officer confirms buyer payout under Art.74.1-1/81-1 -> release seller payment`.

New market evidence:
- Aqrarkredit's United Textiles FZE claim moved from an announced USD **23.109m** initial sale value in May 2026 to USD **19.643m** in the July–August 2026 competition: roughly a **15% announced price reduction**.
- Cabinet rules expressly permit institutional claim-sale discounts down to 70% in first competition and 50% in second competition, with additional flexibility in a third round for weak-recovery assets.
- This proves below-prior-value claim sales are institutionally real in Azerbaijan, but the advertised claim is not shown to be prefunded/distributable and is therefore not a SUCCESS candidate.

Current blocker:
- no concrete `already credited + fully distributable + offered below face` claim has yet been located;
- a real case still needs written confirmation of amount, priority/deductions, Article-31 successor recognition and payout destination before buyer funds become irreversible.

Status: **MECHANISM VALIDATED IN PRINCIPLE / STRONGEST CURRENT LEAD / LIVE INSTANCE GATED / NOT SUCCESS**.

# H102 — prefunded statutory refund receivables: NEW CLASS
Files:
- `research/h102_prefunded_refund_receivables.md`
- `research/CHECKED_PROJECTS_AND_TESTS_H102_APPEND.md`

Validated current examples:
1. **Enforcement e-auction losing-bid deposits:** current `herrac.gov.az` terms require refundable deposits to be returned within **5 working days** after non-winning/other refundable status.
2. **State-property auction deposits:** refundable after non-participation/non-win/cancellation via application to the payer's bank account.
3. **Excess/incorrect state duty:** statutory government refund receivable exists after qualifying overpayment/error.

Why this class matters:
- cash is already prefunded;
- amount is fixed;
- maturity is short;
- ordinary debtor default risk is largely replaced by administrative-recognition risk.

Why it is not SUCCESS:
- public rules currently tie payout to the original participant/payer;
- general Civil Code assignment supports transferability in principle, but no operator-specific assignee-recognition/redirection procedure was found;
- no concrete `unconditional refund + below-face seller + operator confirms assignee payout before seller payment` instance was located.

Status: **MECHANISM CLASS VALIDATED / TRANSFERABILITY + LIVE DISCOUNT INSTANCE GATED / NOT SUCCESS**.
