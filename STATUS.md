# H098 — Azerbaijan statutory finder / treasure / archive rewards: COMPLETED THIS PACKET
Files:
- `research/h098_azerbaijan_statutory_finders_treasure_archive_rewards.md`
- `research/CHECKED_PROJECTS_AND_TESTS_H098_APPEND.md`

Three genuine legal reward mechanisms were validated:
1. **ordinary lost-property finder reward** — Civil Code Article 186 allows a properly reporting finder to demand up to **5% of item value** plus storage costs; standalone guarantee rejected because the qualifying find is stochastic and the exact reward floor is not fixed;
2. **cultural/scientific treasure (`dəfinə`)** — Civil Code Article 187 gives property owner + lawful finder together a statutory reward equal to **50% of the treasure's value** when it must be transferred to the state; economically strong but discovery is stochastic and lawful search/valuation cannot be locked before search cost;
3. **valuable discovered archive documents** — Cabinet Decision No. 32, still listed by the National Archive Administration in 2026, says the Expert-Valuation Commission determines the document value and the **full appraised price** is paid to the discoverer/finder as reward from budgeted funds.

The archive rule is the strongest new H098 lead because reward equals 100% of official appraisal. However, the public procedure does not provide a binding pre-purchase valuation/eligibility quote. A purchase-then-redeem strategy therefore has a legal branch where the Commission values the document below acquisition cost or rejects it.

Strict H098 execution gate:
`binding pre-acquisition reward R_min - acquisition/tax/transaction cost C_max > 0`, with ownership, provenance, eligibility and payout entitlement locked before irreversible payment.

Current 2026 battery-collection notices were also checked; they confirm active collection but no public cash bounty to contributors.

Status: **REAL STATUTORY BOUNTY CLASS VALIDATED / ARCHIVE FULL-APPRAISAL LEAD IDENTIFIED / PRE-VALUATION EXECUTION-GATED / NOT SUCCESS**.

Files:
- `research/h096_azerbaijan_employment_selfemployment_subsidy_screen.md`
- `research/CHECKED_PROJECTS_AND_TESTS_H096_APPEND.md`

Current official programs screened:
1. liberated-territory compulsory social-insurance subsidy — 100% for qualifying self-employed own contributions through 2032; 80% employer contribution reimbursement in 2026–2028;
2. liberated-territory utility support — 20% of qualifying electricity/gas/water/wastewater spend;
3. social-workplace wage co-financing — 50% of salary + specified payroll deductions, subject to cap/approval;
4. 2026 Employment Support Project pilot — first 3 months 100% wages/taxes/deductions, next 3 months 50% for qualifying beneficiaries who hire workers;
5. self-employment program — in-kind materials/equipment/property for one year, transferred to participant ownership after compliant monitored business-plan execution.

Necessary-condition result: reimbursement-only schemes with rate `r<=1` on the same mandatory cost `C` have direct cash floor `-(1-r)C<=0`. Therefore the 100% social-insurance and first-three-month payroll mechanisms can at best reduce the reimbursed cost itself to zero; 80%/50%/20% variants remain negative before productive output.

The **self-employment asset-transfer program remains the strongest H096 lead**, refined by H097 to specific packages. Strict SUCCESS still requires specific approved participant + signed contract + exact delivered assets + locked one-year cost floor + conservative realizable value.
