# H101 — prefunded execution-service deposit claim assignment: COMPLETED THIS PACKET
Files:
- `research/h101_prefunded_execution_service_deposit_claim_assignment.md`
- `research/CHECKED_PROJECTS_AND_TESTS_H101_APPEND.md`

This is now the strongest legal deterministic-cash structure found in the project.

Key mechanics validated from current Azerbaijan law:
1. `İcra haqqında` Article 31 expressly provides procedural substitution when a claim is assigned (`tələb güzəşt edildikdə`).
2. Article 81-1 requires enforcement-document money to move through the state enforcement-service deposit account, tied to the specific enforcement case number.
3. Once payable money has entered that account, Article 81-1.3 provides for disbursement in the claimant's favor on application/bank details.
4. Therefore a buyer can in principle acquire an **already-funded** enforcement claim and be substituted as claimant before seller payment.

Strict theorem:
`cleared distributable deposit - surviving deductions/defenses - purchase price - transfer/tax/bank costs - residual reversal reserve > 0`.

Atomic closing requirement:
`verify cleared funds -> verify distributable amount/no diversion -> execute assignment -> enforcement officer issues Art.31 substitution -> officer confirms payout to buyer -> release seller payment`.

Why H101 is stronger than H100:
- H100 relied on general assignment + notary-deposit mechanics.
- H101 has an enforcement statute that explicitly recognizes claim-assignment succession **inside the live proceeding** and a statutory case-specific deposit payout process.

Live-market control:
- A current Aqrarkredit 2026 competition confirms formal sale of debt claims exists in Azerbaijan.
- The advertised claim is not shown to be already funded into an enforcement deposit account, so it is not a SUCCESS candidate.

Current blocker:
- no concrete `already credited + fully distributable + offered below face` claim has yet been located;
- a real case still needs written confirmation of amount, priority/deductions, Article-31 successor recognition and payout destination before buyer funds become irreversible.
