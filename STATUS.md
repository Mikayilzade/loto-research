## NEW — H067b removes the main paper-authenticity bottleneck
The governing law provides a safer structure than buying an already-issued certificate from an unknown seller:
1. private owner or authorized representative permanently deregisters and surrenders the vehicle;
2. registered utilizer accepts the vehicle and issues the official confirmation document **within 2 working days**;
3. buyer's purchase price is contractually payable **only after official issuance**;
4. buyer receives the original unnamed certificate and redeems it through NVU/Təmiz Şəhər.

Because the certificate is unnamed and another person may use it, this structure avoids making irreversible payment before the official document exists. It therefore substantially reduces counterfeit/double-use risk without requiring a public serial-validation API.

Current operator workflow confirms that the presenter adds certificate information in `nvu.gov.az`, submits the original certificate + ID + own bank details, and the operator transfers the one-time payment within **30 working days** after submission.

## Tax gate narrowed
The Vehicle Utilization Fund is a targeted fund inside the Azerbaijan state budget. State Tax Service guidance on Tax Code Article 102.1.4 states that qualifying individual one-time payments/material assistance financed from state-budget funds under Azerbaijan law/state decisions are exempt from personal income tax.

This gives a **strong exemption argument** for the statutory utilization one-time payment itself. However no Tax Service ruling specifically applying Article 102.1.4 to utilization-certificate redemption was found, so terminal SUCCESS still cannot hard-code tax at zero. Repeated certificate trading could also create separate entrepreneurial-tax issues.
