Exact identity:
`m_break_even_g = 1000 * ask_per_unit / buyer_rate_per_kg`.

- 0.20 AZN ask -> **1.149 g/unit**;

- 3 AZN -> 17.241 g/unit;
- 5 AZN -> 28.736 g/unit.

`m_avg_g > 1000 * (A + F/n) / P`.
