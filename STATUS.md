- M1/M1G or N1/N1G: **1,050 AZN**;
- M2/N2/T: **1,400 AZN**;

Fresh market evidence still includes the Lalafo `900 now / 1,050 after one month` utilization offer, demonstrating an observed **150-AZN below-face liquidity/time discount**.

## Issuance-contingent transaction design remains preferred
For a private eligible vehicle:
1. owner/authorized representative permanently deregisters and surrenders the vehicle;
2. registered utilizer accepts it and issues the official confirmation document within 2 working days;
3. buyer's purchase price is payable only after official issuance;
4. buyer receives the original unnamed certificate and redeems through NVU/Təmiz Şəhər.

This reduces counterfeit/double-use risk because payment can occur after the official document exists.
